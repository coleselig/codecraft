Our story

A family farm since 1850.

A seventh generation Tennessee Century Farm, in continuous livestock production since it was established in 1850. About 200 acres in Greene County, farmed today by the 6th and 7th generations.

  • 1850The farm is established and livestock production begins.
  • 2013The family transitions from cattle to Katahdin sheep.
  • TodayBreeding Katahdins and raising lamb, licensed by TDA for retail meat sales.
