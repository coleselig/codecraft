What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves today. All of them are quick fixes.

1Two different phone numbers are out there

What we saw

Your Square booking page lists (513) 417-9908.Square booking page: square.site/book/VPZ2RYRTW72X8/singler-s-barbershop-cumberland-gap-tn

The Town of Cumberland Gap page lists (423) 441-8887.Town of Cumberland Gap listing: townofcumberlandgap.com/singlers-barber-shop/

Why it matters

Someone who wants to call ahead gets a different number depending on where they look, and one of the two may not reach you. The 513 number is also an Ohio area code, which can make a local caller pause.

Easy fix

Decide which number customers should call and put that same one on Square, the town listing and your own site.

2Saturday hours don't match

What we saw

Square: Saturday: 7:00 AM - 12:00 PM.Square booking page: square.site/book/VPZ2RYRTW72X8/singler-s-barbershop-cumberland-gap-tn

Town page: Sat: 9AM - 5PM.Town of Cumberland Gap listing: townofcumberlandgap.com/singlers-barber-shop/

Why it matters

Saturday is when a lot of people can finally get in for a cut. If one listing is out of date, somebody planning an afternoon visit could drive over to a closed shop, or skip an early slot they didn't know about.

Easy fix

Confirm your real Saturday hours and update whichever listing is wrong. Square's business hours are under Settings → Locations.

3Your own website doesn't show the basics yet

What we saw

singlersbarbershop.com is a Square Online page titled Appointments | Singler’s Barbershop, with the description Haircuts and Shaves. We didn't find your hours, phone number, address or list of services on it.

singlersbarbershop.com (checked with and without www)
Why it matters

The town page prints www.singlersbarbershop.com as your website, so that's where people go to check when you're open or what a shave costs. Right now they have to hunt for that on other pages instead.

Easy fix

Add one short page with your 13 services, one set of hours, the Brooklyn St address, the right phone number and a Book button.

4Search engines get very little to work with

What we saw

The page title (the words on the browser tab, and usually the headline when the site shows up in Google) is Appointments | Singler’s Barbershop. It doesn't mention Cumberland Gap or barbering, and the description is only three words. The site's sitemap file, which lists pages for search engines, is empty.

singlersbarbershop.com and singlersbarbershop.com/sitemap.xml
Why it matters

Someone searching for a barber in Cumberland Gap or Harrogate can't tell from that headline where you are or what you offer.

Easy fix

Change the title to something like “Singler’s Barbershop | Haircuts & Straight Razor Shaves in Cumberland Gap, TN”, write a one-sentence description, and publish the pages so they show up in the sitemap.

What's working

  • Your booking page is clear. Square lists 13 services, from a Kids Cut and Senior Cut to the Straight Razor Head Shave and Face Shave, each with a price and how long it takes.
  • Your address is consistent. Both listings agree on 704 Brooklyn St, Unit 2, Cumberland Gap, TN 37724.
  • Your story is a good one. The town page tells people you're a Master Barber, an LMU graduate trained at The Master Academy of Barbering in Knoxville, and that walk-ins are welcome with appointments guaranteed. That's exactly what belongs on your own site.
