Clean Bee Cleaning Services

We read through your Wix site on November 18, 2026, the way someone in Cleveland looking for a cleaner would. Your hours, phone, email and address are all listed, and the Book Online page lets people pick a service with a set price and book it themselves, which a lot of small businesses don't offer. Here are a few things that would help the site work as hard as you do.

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1The Shop page is still full of Wix sample products

What we saw

Shop is in your main menu. The page lists 12 items, every one named I'm a product, priced from $7.50 to $130.00, with badges like Best Seller and Sale. The same sample products also appear on the home page.

Why it matters

Visitors can't tell what these items are, and sample products with real-looking prices can make people unsure whether the rest of the site is current.

Quick fix

Delete the sample products in the Wix Stores dashboard and hide the Shop page (and the product gallery on the home page) until you have something to sell.

2The social media icons go to Wix, not to you

What we saw

The Facebook, Twitter and LinkedIn icons link to facebook.com/wix, twitter.com/Wix and linkedin.com/company/wix-com, which are Wix's own accounts.

Why it matters

Someone who wants to see your photos or reviews ends up on a website company's page instead, and may not come back.

Quick fix

In the Wix editor, click the social bar and change each link to your own page, or delete any icon you don't use.

3The plan details contradict each other

What we saw

On Plans & Pricing, the Residential Biweekly plan says Every week. The Rental/Moving-in-out and Commercial Property plans each say both Every week and Valid for 3 weeks. One plan reads Book two sessions and the third sessions is 25% off.

Why it matters

This is the page where people decide what to buy. If a biweekly plan says “every week,” a customer can't be sure what they're paying for.

Quick fix

Change the biweekly plan to “Every 2 weeks,” make sure each plan's frequency matches how you actually schedule it, and fix the line to “the third session is 25% off.”

4The only testimonial is your own background, not a customer's

What we saw

The testimonial is signed Natalie Beckler and reads like a résumé: I worked three houses in Cleveland Country Club few years ago. … Took time off due to change of address.

Why it matters

Your experience is a real selling point, but in a testimonial spot people expect a customer's words. A few lines from happy clients carry much more weight.

Quick fix

Move your experience into the About section, and ask two or three clients (you mention a property owner with rentals in TN and GA) if you can quote them here.

5A typo in your services heading

What we saw

The home page heading reads Professional Residential and Comercial Services.

Why it matters

A misspelling in a big heading is one of the first things people notice, especially business customers you'd like to win for commercial work.

Quick fix

Change it to “Commercial.” It takes about ten seconds in the Wix editor.

6A Wix ad banner on top and a 2019 footer

What we saw

The first thing on the page is This website was built on Wix. Create yours today.Get Started, and every page ends with ©2019 by Clean Bee Cleaning Services. Proudly created with Wix.com. The address is natbeckler100.wixsite.com/cleanbeecleaningserv.

Why it matters

The banner pushes your name down and invites visitors to click away, and an old year can make people wonder whether you're still in business. A long wixsite address is also hard to remember or print on a card.

Quick fix

Update the footer to 2026, and if you can, connect a short domain of your own and remove the free-plan banner.

What's working

  • Customers can book themselves. Book Online lists Residential Weekly, Deep Clean, Rentals/Moving, New construction clean up and Residential Biweekly, each with a price and a Book Now button.
  • Your prices are up front. You explain how quotes work (Fair Prices based on level of work and and amount of time) and publish set prices like $100 weekly and $250 for a deep clean.
  • You're easy to find. Your hours (Mon–Fri 9am–6pm, Sat 10am–6pm), phone 423-244-1860, email and Cleveland address are all on the site.
