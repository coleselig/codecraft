How we grow

Soil first, always

Hillside Haven follows organic (not certified), GMO-free, permaculture and regenerative practices.

Nothing synthetic

No chemical fertilizers, pesticides, herbicides or additives go on the crops.

Living soil

Composting, rotational and companion planting, and regular soil tests keep the ground healthy.

Longer seasons

High tunnels extend the growing season, which is how the CSA can run from April into December.

Open to everyone

Woman-owned and Spanish-speaking. Credit/debit, EBT/SNAP and SNAP Incentives accepted.
