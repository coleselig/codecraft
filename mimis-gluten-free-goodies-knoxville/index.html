How to order

Three steps to something warm.

Everything is made fresh to order — so it all starts with a quick note to Mimi.

1

Message Mimi

Tell her what you're craving and any dietary needs. Email or Facebook both work.

2

Confirm the details

Mimi gets back to you to settle what you're ordering, today's price and timing.

3

Pick up & enjoy

Meet Mimi

Gluten-free since 2004.

Mimi has been gluten-free herself since 2004, so she knows what a good gluten-free bake should be: great taste, great texture, wholesome ingredients. Her goodies are made for the whole table, not only for the people who have to skip gluten.

  • Always gluten-freeEvery single bake — not a “gluten-friendly” option.
  • Dairy-free or tailoredCan be made dairy-free or tailored to other dietary needs.
  • Made fresh to orderUsing wholesome, delicious ingredients.

I am a passionate baker with a commitment to helping our gluten free community!

— MimiKnoxville, Tennessee
