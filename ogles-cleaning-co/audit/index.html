Ogles Cleaning Co.

We read through your website on November 17, 2026, page by page, the way a cabin owner looking for a cleaner would. Your phone, email and service area are easy to find, which is great. Here are a few small things that would help the site look as spotless as the cabins you turn over.

Checked November 17, 20265 things to tidy upNo cost, no strings

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site. Most are quick fixes.

1A few typos in the service descriptions

What we saw

On the home page, Rental Cleanings says We take care of the mess so your guest… and Rental Inspections says a report on your rentals condition. The Our Services page also says your rentals maintenance needs.

Why it matters

Cabin owners are trusting you with properties worth a lot of money, and attention to detail is exactly what they're hiring a cleaner for. Small slips in the copy are the kind of thing they notice.

Quick fix

Quick edit pass: your guest → your guests, rentals condition → rental’s condition, rentals maintenance → rental’s maintenance.

2The social media icons go to Wix, not to you

What we saw

The Facebook, Twitter and Instagram icons at the bottom of the page link to facebook.com/wix, twitter.com/wix and instagram.com/wix, the website builder's own accounts.

Why it matters

An owner who taps them to see your work ends up on a software company's page instead of yours, and it's a sign the template was never finished.

Quick fix

Point the icons to your own Facebook or Instagram page, or remove any you don't use.

3A Wix ad is the first thing visitors see

What we saw

The top of both pages we checked shows a Wix advertising banner (Create your website today) with its own sign-up button. The site lives at oglescleaning.wixsite.com/ogles-cleaning-co.

Why it matters

The very first button on your site advertises another company. The long address is also harder to say out loud, print on a card or remember than a simple name.

Quick fix

Upgrading to a paid Wix plan removes the banner and lets you connect your own domain (something like your business name dot com), or move to a site that includes one.

4Google sees your home page as just “HOME”

What we saw

The home page title, the text shown on the browser tab and as the headline in search results, is HOME | Ogles Cleaning Co, and the page has no meta description.

Why it matters

Someone searching for cabin cleaning in Pigeon Forge or Gatlinburg gets no hint from that headline about what you do or where, and without a description Google picks a snippet on its own.

Quick fix

Change the title to something like “Cabin & Rental Cleaning in Sevier County, TN | Ogles Cleaning Co.” and add a one-sentence description (Wix has this under the page’s SEO settings).

5The footer still says 2021

What we saw

Both pages we checked end with © 2021 by Ogles Cleaning Co. All Rights Reserved.

Why it matters

An old year can make people wonder whether the business and the info on the site are still current.

Quick fix

Update it to 2026, or set it to update automatically each year.

What's working

  • Easy to contact. Your phone number (865-705-2440) and email sit at the very top of the page, and both are tap-to-call and tap-to-email on a phone.
  • Clear service area. “Sevier County TN (Sevierville, Pigeon Forge, Gatlinburg)” tells rental owners right away whether you cover their cabin.
  • Services are simple to scan. Rental Cleanings, Rental Maintenance and Rental Inspections are laid out side by side, and the eco-friendly products are a nice touch.
