Free website check-up for Khaos Cleaning Solutions

Hi Leeanne, we're UTK students who build websites for local businesses. We read your site on Nov 17, 2026 and wrote down what a new customer would run into. Everything below is quoted from the live pages.

What we found

1The site lives on a free Wix address with a Wix ad

What we saw
The site is at khaoscleaningsolut.wixsite.com/khaos, and a banner reads “This site was designed with the .com website builder” with a “Start Now” button.
Why it mattersA cut-off web address and someone else's ad make the business look less established than it is.
FixConnect your own domain and remove the builder banner.

2The About page doesn't talk about you or cleaning

What we saw
/about opens with “I began my professional journey in 2000 and have since worked on a number of solo and collaborative projects.” It doesn't mention cleaning, Athens or your name.
Why it mattersPeople letting someone into their home want to know who's coming, and this text doesn't tell them.
FixReplace it with your real story. Your homepage message about people falling behind on housework would be a good start.

3Three different ways to book

What we saw
Book Online says “Khaos Solutions is integrating to using Setmore for all bookings,” but also offers Square (“A credit card is required to book an appointment.”) and Wix booking.
Why it mattersWhen a customer has to pick between three systems, some of them won't book at all.
FixPick one booking system and give every “Book Now” button the same link.

4Prices are on a different page from the services

What we saw
Book Online lists 10 services, each with “Book Now” and “Explore Plans,” but shows no prices. The prices are on a separate page, “Plans & Pricing,” that sits in a sub-menu.
Why it mattersPeople who can't see a price where they're deciding often leave to check somewhere else.
FixShow a starting price beside each service on the booking page.

5The menu has 10+ items

What we saw
The menu lists Home, Contact, Book Online, Blog, Instagram, About, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Code of Conduct, “We Don't Do That” and More.
Why it mattersOn a phone, the important links (Book, Contact) are buried among the policy pages.
FixKeep about 4 links in the menu (Services, Book, About, Contact) and move the policy pages to the footer.

6The site looks like it stopped in 2021

What we saw
The newest blog post is “10 Tips to Simplify Laundry Day,” dated Jul 16, 2021, and the footer reads “©2021 by Khaos Cleaning Solutions.”
Why it mattersVisitors who see old dates may wonder whether the business is still taking jobs.
FixUpdate the footer year and post something recent, or hide the dates.

What's working

Your phone number, (423) 453-3140, is easy to find, and the “Get In Touch” button calls it directly. Your “We Don't Do That” page tells people up front what you won't take on, so nobody is surprised later.
