Why this business exists
Falling behind on housework happens to everyone. Asking for help is the first step back to calm.

Lots of adults look like they have it all handled, but grief, depression or plain lack of motivation can let the house slip. Cleaning can feel overwhelming or it can be therapeutic, and Khaos Cleaning Solutions was started to help people live a better, cleaner life. No judgment, just help.
