How booking works.

Touch of Beauty is a home-based studio, so the street address isn’t posted online. Here’s exactly what happens after you book.

01

Pick the exact service you need on Square. Each one has its own time allotment, and a non-refundable deposit holds your spot.

02

The studio address is texted to you 24 hours before your appointment. Keep an eye on your phone.

03

Arrive & relax

Come solo (no extra guests or children), settle in, and raise any concerns before you leave the table.

Before you book

The fine print, made friendly.

  • Deposits are non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • Up to three refills are allowed before a removal and full set is required. Refills that turn into full sets count toward the three.
  • No work over another artist’s extensions. Please book a removal and new set.
  • No additional guests or children at appointments.
  • Book the specific service you need, since each has a different time allotment.
  • Have a concern? Bring it up before leaving the table.
