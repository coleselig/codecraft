Pick your own tulips in Sparta

A family u-pick tulip farm just outside Cookeville. Come walk the rows in early spring, fill your arms with stems, and stay for the markets and farm days the Roberts family hosts through the year.

Before you come

A u-pick morning, done right

Tulips are picked in early spring, roughly March into April, and bloom time always depends on the weather. Opening day and field updates go out on the farm's Facebook and Instagram first.

Wear boots

The rows are real fields. Boots, or at least closed-toe shoes, make the walk a lot more fun.

Check bloom first

Spring weather moves the calendar. Peek at the latest post before you load up the car.

Priced by the stem

There's a small admission, and tulips are priced per stem. Current prices are posted with each season's opening.
