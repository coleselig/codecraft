Free website check-up for We Can Do It Cleaning Service

We looked at wecandoitcleaning.wixsite.com/weclean and its Services page on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1There's no phone number anywhere

Saw Neither the home page nor the Services page shows a phone number. The only contact listed is wecandoitcleaningservicetn@outlook.com, even though the form asks visitors for their Phone Number*.

Cost Many people booking a cleaner want to call or text first. Without a number, some will move on to a company they can call.

Fix Add your number to the header and footer as a tap-to-call link.

2The home page "Our Services" section doesn't name any services

Saw On the home page, Our Services is followed by Reliable & Honest, which describes your team but doesn't name a single service. Your six services (Bi-Weekly Home Cleaning, Move-In Move-Out Cleaning, Complete Home Deep Clean, Market Ready Cleaning, Office Cleaning, After Event Cleaning) only show on the separate Services page.

Cost A visitor who stays on the home page can't tell whether you do what they need, such as a move-out or office clean.

Fix Show those six services, with one line each, right in the home page "Our Services" section.

3The estimate form's Service Type shows no choices

Saw The home page Contact for Estimate form has a required Service Type* field, and we couldn't see any service choices listed for it.

Cost If a required field has nothing to pick, a customer may not be able to send the form, and you'd never hear from them.

Fix Fill the Service Type list with your six services (plus "Other"), then send yourself a test inquiry to make sure it arrives.

4Your service area isn't spelled out

Saw The footer location says Service area specific to client request, though the home page says you've been servicing the Cumberland county area since 2018.

Cost People in nearby towns want to know right away whether you come to them.

Fix Name the towns you cover (for example Crossville and the rest of Cumberland County) in the footer and near the top.

What's working

The site is current (© 2026), lists clear hours (Mon — Fri: 8am — 4pm), has a real customer review from Barbara Glenn, a Request a Quote button, and links to your own Facebook page. That's a solid base to build on.
