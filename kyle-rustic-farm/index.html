From the farm store

What’s raised and grown here

Meat, eggs, produce and pantry goods from the farm in Paint Rock Valley. What’s on hand changes with the season, so call ahead for this week’s list.

Pasture-raised meats

Beef, pork, lamb, chicken, duck and turkey, raised outdoors with rotational grazing. Custom beef, pork, lamb and poultry.

Eggs

Farm eggs from hens on pasture.

Raw milk drop point

The farm is a drop point for fresh raw milk. Call for pickup details.

Seasonal produce

Tomatoes, pole beans, okra, peppers, cucumbers, potatoes, sweet potatoes, pumpkins, muscadines, elderberries, herbs and edible flowers.

Pantry

Honey, bee pollen, jams, jellies and spreads, maple syrup, and lard, leaf lard and fat for rendering.

Barnwood & more

Barnwood guitars, firewood, candles and a seed exchange.

How we farm

Nestled in the hollow of Paint Rock Valley

A pasture-based, family-friendly farm using rotational grazing, where the animals are raised outdoors on open pasture.

  • Rotational grazingLivestock move across open pasture.
  • No chemicals, no hormonesNo chemical fertilizers, pesticides, herbicides, antibiotics or growth hormones.
  • Room for pollinatorsTrees, grasses, legumes and flowers planted for pollinator habitat.
