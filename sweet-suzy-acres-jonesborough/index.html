How Sweet Suzy Acres raises them

Every step happens here.

Sweet Suzy Acres is a farrow-to-finish farm: hogs are born, raised and finished on the farm, with feeder pigs brought in some of the time.

01

Farrow

Piglets born on the farm, the start of a farrow-to-finish operation.

02

Rotate

Hogs moved across the pasture in rotation, caring for the land as well as the animals.

03

Feed

Non-GMO grain, grown and milled right here in the area.

04

Finish

Retail pork for local families, on the farm and at the Jonesborough Farmers Market.
