Store directory

Come in and see what turns up.

Six sections of merchandise plus guest vendors, so the room keeps changing. Here’s roughly where things are.

01

Thrift & vintage clothing

Clothes from the 1960s through the 1990s, plus a few current pieces, priced from $2 up to $1,000.

02

Vintage shoes

A longtime specialty for the shop: footwear picked with a reseller’s eye.

03

Guitars & equipment

Guitars and related gear. The shop plans to grow this section.

04

Art & oddities

Odd and strange objects from a collector’s shelves, shown next to original artwork.

05

Hats, tees, jackets & sweaters

Everyday layers and statement outerwear for whatever the weather does next.

06

Card games & trinkets

Card games and small collectibles. Good for gifts and for anyone who likes to dig.
