Free website check-up for Goforth Residential Cleaning Services

We looked at goforthcleaningser.wixsite.com/website on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1One "Get in Touch" button emails a misspelled address

Saw The Get in Touch button under MOVE IN & OUT CLEANING opens an email to gofrthcleaningservices@gmail.com (the "o" after "g" is missing). Your other buttons and your contact section use goforthcleaningservices@gmail.com.

Cost Someone asking about a move-out clean from that button would be writing to an address you don't receive, and you'd never know they tried.

Fix Point that button at goforthcleaningservices@gmail.com, or better, at a short quote form that lands in your inbox.

2The booking page lists 8-minute and 5-minute cleans

Saw The Book Online page shows residential cleaning services · 8 min · Prices vary on job, and Move In & Out Cleaning · 5 min · Prices vary.

Cost A customer reading "5 min" for a move-out clean may be confused about what they're booking, and could pick a slot far too short for the job.

Fix Set realistic times for each service, or swap online booking for "Call or text for a free estimate".

3A Wix ad is the first thing at the top

Saw This site was designed with the .com website builder. Create your website today. with a Start Now button that links to Wix.

Cost The first button a visitor sees sends them to someone else's sign-up page instead of to you.

Fix Move to a site without the ad banner and put a tap-to-call button in that spot.

4Social icons point to Wix, not to you

Saw The Facebook, Twitter and LinkedIn icons in the footer go to facebook.com/wix, twitter.com/Wix and linkedin.com/company/wix-com.

Cost People who tap them hoping to see your reviews or photos end up on Wix's pages.

Fix Link your own pages if you have them, or remove the icons.

5Footer still says ©2019

Saw ©2019 by Goforth Residential Cleaning Services. Proudly created with Wix.com

Cost An old date can make people wonder whether you're still taking new customers.

Fix Show the current year, and replace the Wix line with your phone number and the towns you serve.

6Two small typos in key spots

Saw The phone number appears as 423) 545-8125 (missing the opening parenthesis), and the about text says a family owed and operated with 19 years of experience.

Cost Small slips in your number and your "about us" line are what careful customers notice when deciding who to let into their home.

Fix Change them to "(423) 545-8125" and "family owned and operated", and make the number a tap-to-call link.

What's working

You already list real services (Move In & Out Cleaning, Window Washing), your experience (19 years, family run), and your full contact details: email, phone and 2665 Fairview rd Madisonville tn 37354. That's a solid base to build on.
