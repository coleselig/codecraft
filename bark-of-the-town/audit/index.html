Bark of the Town

We read through your website and your Pawfinity client page on November 18, 2026, the way a pet owner near McDonald and Cleveland looking for a groomer would. Your phone, email and address are easy to find, and you list real prices. Here are a few small things that would help the site show off the care you put into every pet.

Checked November 18, 20265 things to tidy upNo cost, no strings

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1The footer still says 2020, under a Wix ad

What we saw

The home page footer reads ©2020 by Bark of the Town. Proudly created with Wix.com, and the top of the page shows the banner This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button.

Why it matters

An old year can make people wonder whether the business and its details are still current, and the first button on the site advertises another company.

Easy fix

Update the footer to 2026 (or remove the year). A paid Wix plan removes the banner and lets you connect barkofthetown.biz.

2Your Pawfinity page says “Coming soon!” for prices and reviews

What we saw

Your website links to your Pawfinity client page. There, the Pricing page and the Reviews page each say only Coming soon!

Why it matters

A new client who goes there to sign up and taps Pricing or Reviews finds empty pages, even though you already list prices on your own Book Online page.

Easy fix

Copy your prices (bath under 25 lbs $25, nail trim $10, nail grind $16) into the Pawfinity Pricing page, or hide the two tabs until they’re filled in.

3No reviews are shown anywhere

What we saw

The home page asks How Was Your Experience? and Would you recommend us to your friends? with a Yes/No form, but no customer reviews are displayed on the site.

Why it matters

People hand their pets only to groomers they trust, and reading what other owners say is how most of them decide. The feedback you collect isn’t being seen by new visitors.

Easy fix

Add three or four short reviews from happy clients (with first names and pet names) to the home page.

4“Learn More” leaves your site

What we saw

The main Learn More button under Bark of the Town Welcomes You With Open Paws links to facebook.com/petstylist85.

Why it matters

Visitors who want to learn more about your grooming are sent away from your site to Facebook, where they may get distracted and never come back to book.

Easy fix

Point “Learn More” to your services and prices, and keep the Facebook icon for people who want to follow you.

5No hours listed

What we saw

The home page lists your address (6217 Tallent Rd, McDonald), email and phone (423-826-9661), but no opening hours or drop-off times. Your Pawfinity page doesn’t list hours either.

Why it matters

Pet owners plan grooming around work. Without hours they have to call just to find out when they can drop off.

Easy fix

Add your hours and drop-off/pick-up times next to your contact details.

What's working

  • Real prices up front. Your Book Online page lists a bath under 25 lbs for $25, nail trims for $10 and nail grinds for $16, which many groomers never publish.
  • Easy to reach. Your phone number is in the header, the “Get in Touch” buttons call you directly, and your address and email are on the home page.
  • Experience that builds trust. “12 years professional experience with pet care & grooming” and a friendly tagline (“Where tails are waggin’ & Pets are braggin’”) set the right tone.
