FAQ

Straight answers.

Who are you?

Code Craft Media is a small web design studio run by students at the University of Tennessee in Knoxville. We build websites for local shops, trades and food spots around East Tennessee.

Do I need a domain?

No. Your site works on free hosting from day one. If you want your own name like yourshop.com, you buy it from any registrar (usually around $12 a year) and we connect it for free.

How long does it take?

Usually a few days after you send us your logo, photos and details.

What do you need from me?

Your business name and town, your logo and a few photos, plus whatever you want on the site: menu, prices, services, hours. Send it by Instagram DM or email.

What if I don't like it?

Then you don't pay. We build it first and you get up to 2 rounds of edits before launch. If it's still not right for you, you owe us nothing.

Do you do calls?

We work by message and email to keep it quick. No meetings to schedule and no sales calls.

When do I pay?

Only after your site is live and you've approved it. Then we send one invoice for the one-time price: $249 for a full site or $149 for a one-page site.

Are there monthly fees?

No. Hosting is free. Changes after launch are $20 each, only when you ask for them.

Do you use AI?

Yes. We use AI tools to help design and build faster, which is a big part of how we can charge a one-time $149 to $249 instead of agency prices. But a person checks every detail, every fact on your site comes from your own pages and info, and you see and approve the finished site before you pay anything.

Why did you build me a site I didn't ask for?

Because showing is easier than telling. A quick example of what your site could look like says more than a sales pitch. There's no obligation at all. If you're not interested, just reply “no thanks” and we won't bother you again.
