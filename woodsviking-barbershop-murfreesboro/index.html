About

A barbershop on the Square

Woodsviking Barbershop is a barbershop on the south side of the Public Square in downtown Murfreesboro, offering haircuts, kid cuts, hot towel shaves, beard trims and line ups.

Appointments are booked online, Tuesday through Saturday, with Stephen behind the chair.
