Why Divine Purity

A family workshop that went all in

Cassandra Tucker started Divine Purity in 2012. Two years later she left her job as a loan processor to sell at the Chattanooga Market, and a year after that her husband Ray joined her full-time. Shops that stock their goods on Faire give the brand a 5.0 rating across 34 reviews.
