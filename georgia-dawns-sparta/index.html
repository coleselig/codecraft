In the shop

Three reasons to step inside

Everything on our shelves falls under one simple promise: goods, gifts and local finds.

i.

Goods

Thoughtfully chosen pieces for everyday life, the kind you pick up on a Main Street stroll and keep for years.

ii.

Gifts

Birthdays, thank-yous, hostess gifts and "just because" — a downtown stop for something with more heart than a big-box aisle.

iii.

Local finds

A home on North Main for local finds, so shopping in Sparta keeps supporting Sparta and the White County community.
