Castro Roofing & Exteriors

We read through your website page by page, the way a Chattanooga homeowner with a leaking roof would. Your phone number and address are easy to find everywhere, which is great. Here are a few small things that would make sure every request actually reaches you.

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site on November 18, 2026. Most are quick fixes you can make in the Wix editor.

1The email link in “Get in Touch” drops the first letter

What we saw

In the Get in Touch section of the home page, the address reads castroroofingandexteriors@gmail.com, but the first c sits outside the link. Tapping it opens an email to astroroofingandexteriors@gmail.com, without the c.

Why it matters

A homeowner who taps that link is writing to an address that isn't yours, so their message may never reach you, and neither of you would know.

Easy fix

Re-select the whole address and link it to castroroofingandexteriors@gmail.com. The footer and Contact page already use the right one.

2The Instagram icon goes to Wix, not to you

What we saw

The Instagram icon links to instagram.com/wix, the website builder's own account. (Your Facebook icon correctly goes to your own page.)

Why it matters

Someone who taps it to see your roofs lands on a software company's page, which makes the site look unfinished.

Easy fix

Point it to your Instagram account, or remove the icon if you don't use Instagram.

3Portfolio pages still show the builder's starter text

What we saw

On the Portfolio page, opening Metal roofing shows the heading Create Your First Project and the line Start adding your projects to your portfolio. Its web address ends in /lead-generation-for-roofing-services.

Why it matters

The portfolio is where homeowners go to check your work before they call. Setup instructions there make it look like the page was never finished.

Easy fix

Delete the starter text, add a few photos and a sentence about each job, and rename the page addresses to match (for example /metal-roofing).

4The Privacy Policy link opens a Wix how-to article

What we saw

The footer links Privacy Policy, Accessibility Statement and Terms & Policy all open the same page (/blank). It holds Wix's guide text, with headings like A legal disclaimer and What to include in the Privacy Policy.

Why it matters

Your quote form asks for names, phone numbers and emails. Anyone who checks how you handle that information finds instructions instead of a policy.

Easy fix

Replace it with a short, plain policy of your own (what you collect and that you only use it to reply), and remove the two links you don't need.

5A Wix ad at the top and a 2023 footer

What we saw

The home page opens with the banner This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. and a Get Started button. Every page ends with © 2023 by Castro Roofing & Exteriors LLC.

Why it matters

The first button on your site advertises another company, and an old year can make people wonder if the site is still kept up.

Easy fix

A paid Wix plan removes the banner and lets you use your own domain name. Update the footer to 2026.

What's working

  • Easy to reach. Your phone number (423-660-1822) and address (1826 Dixon Street, Chattanooga) are on every page we checked.
  • A smart contact form. Asking for the Reason for the inspection (ex. Roof leaking, insurance inspection) means you know what the job is before you call back.
  • You show your range. The portfolio names metal, asphalt, EPDM and TPO roofing, so both homeowners and commercial customers can see you handle their roof type.
