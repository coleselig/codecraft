Our story

Six generations of Henards.

  1. 1899

    James O. Henard starts the business with a sawmill in the Klondike community.

  2. 1932

    His son, A.E. Henard, buys the operation, and in the 1940s it moves to Henard Town.

  3. 1965

    Ross Henard buys the business, closes the sawmill and builds a modern store.

  4. Today

    The family still runs the store, and the old building is used as warehouse space.

Stop in and look around: the original Klondike sign and antique gas pumps are part of the store.As reported by The Rogersville Review
