Heavy Duty Contracting

We read through your website page by page, the way a homeowner or rental investor in East Tennessee would. Your phone number is right at the top and your blog is full of useful posts, which is great. Here are a few small things that would make it easier for people to reach you.

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site on November 18, 2026. Most are quick fixes you can make in the Wix editor.

1“Email us” shows a web address instead of an email

What we saw

In the contact section of the home page, under EMAIL US, the site shows heavydutycontracting.com. It isn't an email address and it isn't a clickable link.

Why it matters

Property managers and investors often prefer to send details by email. Right now there's no address for them to write to.

Easy fix

Put your real email address there and link it (in Wix: Link → Email) so it opens a new message when tapped.

2The Facebook icon goes to Wix, not to you

What we saw

The Facebook icon links to facebook.com/wix, the website builder's own page.

Why it matters

Customers who tap it to see your recent jobs end up on a software company's page instead.

Easy fix

Point it to your business Facebook page, or remove it.

3A typo and odd details on the Book Online page

What we saw

The first service is listed as pressure wasing/soft washing, and every service, including Excavation and Skidsteer grading, shows 1 hr.

Why it matters

A typo in the first line of your booking page is easy to notice, and 1 hr next to excavation can read like you're promising the whole job in an hour.

Easy fix

Fix the spelling, capitalize the service names to match, and if the hour is for the quote visit, rename them something like “Free quote visit: Excavation.”

4A Wix ad, a 2021 footer and a cut-off name

What we saw

Pages open with the banner This site was designed with the .com website builder. Create your website today. The footer reads © 2021 by Heavy Duty Contracting. All rights reserved. The Blog page's browser tab says Blog | Heavy Duty Contracti.

Why it matters

The first button on your site advertises another company, and an old year or a cut-off name can make people wonder if the site is kept up. Your blog posts run into 2024, so the business is clearly active.

Easy fix

A paid Wix plan removes the banner and lets you use your own domain. Update the footer to 2026 and change the site name to the full “Heavy Duty Contracting” in Settings.

What's working
