How to order

From idea to "wow" in four sweet steps

Here's how ordering works, straight from Carrie's terms. The earlier you reach out, the better.

  1. 1

    Reach out

    Call, email or send a message with your date, your questions and any reference pictures you have.

    931-644-4428
  2. 2

    Design together

    Book an appointment to design your custom cake with Carrie and get your quote.

    Every cake is custom
  3. 3

    Save your date

    Your date is booked with a 50% non-refundable deposit (100% if booked 15 days or less out). Changes are welcome up to 15 days before.

    Final payment 2 days before
  4. 4

    Pick up & enjoy

    Pick up your cake, or have it delivered to your venue. Your care and cutting guide comes with it. Time to enjoy!

    Delivery from $100, by venue
