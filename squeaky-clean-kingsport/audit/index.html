Free website check-up

Squeaky Clean & More

We read through your website page by page, the way a Kingsport family looking for a cleaner would. Your email, location and Facebook page are easy to find, which is great. Here are a few small things that would help the site look as spotless as the homes you clean.

Checked November 18, 20266 things to tidy upNo cost, no strings

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site on November 18, 2026. Most are quick fixes you can make in the Wix editor.

1The booking page says you serve San Francisco

What we saw

On the Book Online page: Squeaky Clean & more is dedicated to providing high quality services to the San Francisco area.

Why it matters

That line came with the template. A Kingsport customer reading it at the moment they're about to book may wonder if they're on the right site.

Easy fix

Change it to Kingsport and the towns you actually cover.

2Only the Basic Clean can be booked

What we saw

Book Online lists Basic Clean with a Request to Book button. Window Washing, Move In & Out Cleaning and Green Cleaning Services are listed without a time, price or booking button.

Why it matters

Move In & Out and Green Cleaning are the two services featured on your home page, but a customer who wants one of them can't book it online.

Easy fix

Add the other services to Wix Bookings (or a “request a quote” option for each), or add a line saying to call or email for those.

3The service descriptions don't describe the services

What we saw

Under MOVE IN & OUT CLEANING on the home page: Since its founding, Squeaky Clean & more has been one of the most trusted names in the industry. Under GREEN CLEANING SERVICES: We have the experience and skills necessary to tackle just about every type of job that comes our way.

Why it matters

These are general template sentences. Someone deciding whether to hire you can't tell what a move-out clean includes or what makes your green cleaning green.

Easy fix

Replace each with 3–4 short bullets, like inside the fridge and oven, baseboards, and the products you use.

4The “More” menu item goes nowhere

What we saw

The top menu shows Home, Book Online and More. More isn't linked to anything and has no pages under it.

Why it matters

Visitors who tap it expect more information and get nothing, which makes the site feel unfinished.

Easy fix

Remove it, or replace it with a Contact or Services link.

5Your phone number is hard to read and not tap-to-call

What we saw

The number appears as 4234462717 in Get in Touch and at the bottom of the page, as plain text rather than a call link.

Why it matters

Ten digits run together are easy to misdial, and on a phone people expect to just tap the number.

Easy fix

Write it as 423-446-2717 and link it as a phone number (in Wix: Link → Phone).

6A Wix ad and a 2020 footer

What we saw

Pages open with the banner This site was designed with the .com website builder. Create your website today. and end with ©2020 by Squeaky Clean & more. Proudly created with Wix.com.

Why it matters

The first button on your site advertises another company, and an old year can make people wonder whether the site is still current.

Easy fix

A paid Wix plan removes the banner and lets you use your own domain. Update the footer to 2026.

What's working

  • A friendly, clear promise. Professional Quality with a personal touch! says what makes you different in a few words.
  • Your Facebook link actually works. The icon goes to your own page (facebook.com/squeakycleanandmore), which a lot of small-business sites get wrong.
  • Easy to email and easy to place. Your email and Kingsport, Tennessee 37660 are right there in Get in Touch, along with a contact form.
