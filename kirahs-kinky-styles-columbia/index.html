Before you book.

Kirah's policies, from her booking page. They keep the calendar fair for everyone and respect her home.

1

Deposits are required

Because of more late cancellations and no-shows, every booking needs a deposit.

2

24-hour notice to change or cancel

Please notify of any change or cancellation 24 hours before your appointment. No-call no-shows are charged a $10 cancellation fee.

3

15-minute grace period

After 15 minutes late, the appointment is cancelled automatically.

4

No same-day appointments

Book at least 24 hours in advance.

5

Confirm when notified

Unconfirmed appointments don't hold your time slot.

6

Come with clean hair

Washes aren't available right now. New clients, please come ready to pay.

7

Arrive on time, not early

It's a home location, so please arrive no more than 5 minutes early and no more than 15 minutes late.

8

Bringing someone? Ask first

Let Kirah know ahead of time if anyone is coming with you, children included.
