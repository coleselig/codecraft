Before you come in

Prep like a pro.

Straight from Melt’s FAQ.

Grow it out

Let hair grow at least 2 weeks, about ¼ inch.

Dress comfy

Wear loose, breathable clothing to your appointment.

Check your skincare

No retinol 7 days before and after; 6 months off Accutane before waxing.

Rebook in 4 weeks

Wax every 4 weeks. Use ingrown oil daily and exfoliate 3× a week.

Good to know: waxing on your period is fine with a tampon, disc or cup, and waxing during pregnancy is OK too.
