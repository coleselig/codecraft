Meet the baker

Grandma's kitchen, Hailey's oven

Hailey's Homemade Bakes started as a small home bakery in Soddy Daisy. Hailey bakes homemade cookies and other goods with organic ingredients, and she wants her baking to give back to her community.

  • Organic ingredientsHailey's own words: baked goods “made with organic ingredients and lots of love.”
  • Over 10 years of bakingA passion that started in the kitchen with her grandma.
  • Short ingredient listsSandwich bread uses just 6 ingredients, and the buns use 8.
  • Made from scratchHomemade in Hailey's own kitchen in Soddy Daisy.
