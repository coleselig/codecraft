Craft barbecue from all over the country, brought home to Knoxville

Pitmaster Eric Pickle found craft barbecue in 2018 while he was traveling for work, eating his way through joints across the U.S. In 2020 he started smoking meat in his backyard on a used offset he bought for $100.

The idea was simple: bring the country's best barbecue styles to East Tennessee. That means Texas beef next to West Tennessee and Carolina pork and chicken, plus sides both classic and new.
