Free website check-up for Cahue Superb Washing

We looked at cahuesuperbwashing.wixsite.com/cahuesuperbwashingll and its Book Online and Shop pages on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1The Shop page is full of template products

Saw Every item on the Shop page is named I'm a product, with labels like Best Seller, New and Sale.

Cost A customer who taps Shop in the menu sees a store that looks unfinished, which can make them doubt the business.

Fix Remove the Shop page from the menu, or replace it with something useful like a photo gallery of your work.

2A typo in the first line customers read

Saw Under Full House Wash: We can wash anything! Vynil, Brick, Stucco.

Cost It's small, but it sits right under your main service, so it's one of the first things a customer reads.

Fix Change "Vynil" to "Vinyl".

3A Wix ad across the top of every page

Saw This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button on all three pages we checked.

Cost The first button a visitor sees sends them to Wix's sign-up page instead of to you.

Fix Move to a site without the ad banner and put "Call for a free quote" in that spot.

4Footer still says ©2023 and "created with Wix"

Saw ©2023 by Cahue Superb Washing,LLC. Proudly created with Wix.com

Cost An old year can make people wonder whether the business is still active.

Fix Show the current year and replace the Wix line with your phone and service area.

5No hours, and the phone number is hard to read

Saw We didn't find business hours on any page, and the footer shows your number as 8652249011.

Cost People can't tell when you'll pick up, and a run-together number is harder to read and dial.

Fix Add your hours and write the number as (865) 224-9011 with a tap-to-call button.

What's working

Your three main services are clearly named with short descriptions, the Get in Touch buttons call you directly, your Book Online page lists a free consultation, and your address, phone and email are all on the home page. That's a solid base to build on.
