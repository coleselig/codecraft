Premier Nails Bearden Station

We read through your website on November 18, 2026, the way someone looking for a new nail salon in Bearden would. Your Services page lists nearly every service with a price, which a lot of salons never do, and it makes choosing easy. Here are a few things that would help the site work as hard as your team does.

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1The Giftcard page says orders can't be processed

What we saw

The Giftcard page in your menu starts with Buy our gift card for your loved one, then says Our system is being upgraded. Your order has not been processed this time. We apologize for this inconvenience. There's no way to buy a gift card on the page, only a number to call.

Why it matters

Gift cards are often a last-minute, evening purchase, and the holidays are coming up. A visitor who reads “your order has not been processed” may think something went wrong and buy a gift somewhere else.

Quick fix

Ask Fastboy to turn the gift card checkout back on. If that will take a while, change the text to something simple like “Gift cards are available at the salon. Call 865-219-1160 or stop by.”

2There's no way to book an appointment on the site

What we saw

On every page we checked (Home, Services, Contact, Giftcard and Best Offers), the only booking option is the CALL NOW button. We didn't find a “Book” link or an online schedule anywhere.

Why it matters

Many people browse for a nail appointment at night or on a break, when they can't call. If they can't grab a time right then, they may book with a salon that lets them do it online.

Quick fix

If you use (or want to use) an online booking tool, add a “Book Now” button next to “CALL NOW.” Even a line like “Text or DM us on Instagram to book” gives after-hours visitors a way in.

3The pedicure packages don't say what's included

What we saw

Under Natural Pedicure, the menu lists Package pedicure 1 $45, Package pedicure 2 $75, Package pedicure 3 $59 and Package pedicure 4 $85. None of them has a description, and they aren't in price order (2 costs more than 3).

Why it matters

Pedicures are something people choose by what they get: the scrub, the massage, the mask. With only numbers and prices, it's hard to tell why one is $45 and another $85, so people may skip the upgrade or call just to ask.

Quick fix

Give each package a short name and one line on what it includes, and list them from lowest to highest price.

4Some home page wording looks like leftover template text

What we saw

Under the Contact heading, the home page says Best Nails Design For You instead of contact details. The line We Have Many Services appears twice, under two Services headings, and the gallery is titled Top Best Ever Finished.

Why it matters

The home page is your first impression. Generic or awkward lines make it feel like a template rather than your salon, and they take the spot where something specific could go.

Quick fix

Replace them with real details: your phone and hours under “Contact,” one “Services” section that names your favorites (Gel X, builder gel, SNS, spa pedicures), and a gallery title like “Recent work from our team.”

5A small spelling slip on the Services page

What we saw

Under Artificial Nails, the shape line reads Almond / Coffin / Stilletto / French. A few lines up, under Additional, the same list spells it Stiletto.

Why it matters

It's minor, but the price menu is the page people read most closely, so small slips stand out there.

Quick fix

Change “Stilletto” to “Stiletto” so both lines match.

What's working

  • Your prices are out in the open. The Services page lists manicures, SNS, Gel X, builder gel, acrylic and waxing with prices, from the Basic Manicure at $30 up.
  • Hygiene is front and center. The home page mentions sterilized implements, single-use buffers and files, and that All pedicure services include liner protection. That's exactly what new clients want to know.
  • You're easy to reach. Your phone, 865-219-1160, address, email and full weekly hours are on every page, and the CALL NOW button taps to call on a phone.
