Free website check-up for HandiWork Ephesians 2:10

We looked at handiworkeph210.wixsite.com/website and its Book Online, Plans & Pricing and Blog pages on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1Three services still say “Service Name”

Saw On the home page and the Book Online page, three of the four services are titled Service Name (one reads 0ervice Name), each 1 hr at $19.99 with no description. Only Handyman is filled in.

Cost Customers can't tell what you actually do, and template text can make them doubt the business is active.

Fix Replace these with real jobs you take on (for example repairs, installs, small remodels) or remove them and keep “Handyman”.

2Plans & Pricing page is empty

Saw The Plans & Pricing page in your menu says No plans available and Once there are plans available for purchase, you'll see them here.

Cost A visitor who clicks to see your prices finds nothing, which can feel like a dead end.

Fix Remove the page from the menu, or replace it with a short “How pricing works / free estimates” page.

3Blog page has no posts

Saw The Blog page shows Check back soon and Once posts are published, you'll see them here.

Cost An empty page in the menu makes the site feel unfinished.

Fix Hide the Blog until you have posts. Your Projects page (Staircase Renovation, Ceiling Support, Attic Door Installation) is a better use of that spot.

4A Wix ad sits at the top of the site

Saw The home page shows This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button that links to Wix.

Cost The first button a visitor sees sends them to Wix's sign-up page instead of to you.

Fix Move to a site without the ad banner and put “Get An Estimate” in that spot.

5Footer still says ©2021

Saw ©2021 by HandiWork Ephesians 2:10. Proudly created with Wix.com

Cost A five-year-old date can make people wonder whether you're still in business.

Fix Show the current year, and replace the Wix line with your phone number and service area.

What's working

Your headline says exactly who you are and where (Your Maryville Handyman), your address (1607 Griffitts Blvd, Maryville, TN 37803), phone (865) 850-1897 and email are on the home page, and the Projects page shows real work: Staircase Renovation, Ceiling Support and Attic Door Installation. That's a solid base to build on.
