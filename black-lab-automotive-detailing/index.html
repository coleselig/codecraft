What we do

From a quick shine to a full paint correction.

Every vehicle and every finish is different, so pricing is quoted per job. Pick a service and a time on the booking page, or call to talk it through.

Paint Correction

Swirls, haze and light scratches leveled out with wet sanding, cut-and-polish or a one-step correction, depending on what your paint needs. Every paint correction includes a minimum 3-year graphene coating to lock the gloss in.

Graphene coating included

Ceramic Coating

A hard, slick protective layer that makes water bead and roll off, and keeps your car easier to wash.

Paint Protection Film

Clear film applied over high-impact areas to guard against rock chips and road debris on mountain roads.

Interior Deep Cleaning

Seats, carpets and every surface you touch, cleaned properly instead of just wiped down.

Detail & Wash

Basic maintenance details and washes to keep a car that's already in good shape looking that way.
