The farm

No middleman. Just a grower and his hostas.

White's Nursery is a working farm on Raccoon Valley Road, not a garden center that ships plants in.

Everything is grown here

Brian doesn't buy in plants to resell. What you take home was propagated and potted on the farm.

Started in 1998, for East Tennessee gardens

Brian started the nursery because hostas weren't available around Knoxville in the size gardeners here needed.

Known as "the Hosta Man"

That's how many local gardeners know Brian.
