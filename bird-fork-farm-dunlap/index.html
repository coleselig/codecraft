Teas, tinctures & herbs grown seed to bottle on the plateau.

Bird Fork Farm is a small, woman owned and operated farmstead outside Dunlap, Certified Naturally Grown since 2020, hand-farming a few acres and protecting the rest as forest and prairie.

From the farm

A seed-to-bottle apothecary farm: produce and herbs grown organically, plus locally grown or foraged teas, spices, extracts, body care and ferments.

Teas & herbs

Herbal teas and fresh and dried herbs, grown or foraged locally.

Tinctures & oxymels

Small-batch extracts, tinctures and oxymels from the farm's herbs.

Body care

Body care products made from what grows on the farm.

Fruit & produce

Apples, blueberries, pears, greens, tomatoes, squash, pumpkins and more in season.

Ferments & spices

Value-added goods: ferments, spices and other pantry staples.

Workshops & CSAs

On-farm workshops on herbalism, homesteading and foraging, plus Herbal CSAs.

Why Bird Fork

Grown the slow way, by hand, on the Cumberland Plateau.

Certified Naturally Grown

Certified Naturally Grown produce since 2020.

Hand-farmed

About 2¼ of 55 acres farmed by hand with regenerative permaculture methods.

Land kept wild

The remaining acreage protected as forest and prairie, with fire re-introduced to the land.

Woman owned

A small, diverse, woman owned and operated farmstead started by a former archaeologist.
