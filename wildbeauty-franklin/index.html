About the studio

One stylist. Your color, start to finish.

WildBeauty is Brittany Small's color studio in Franklin — Studio #10 inside 615 Baker's Bridge Ave. Her signature is lived-in custom color designed to grow out softly between visits.

  • Every color service includes a consultation, shampoo and blow dry.
  • Free consultations — required before corrective color and extensions.
  • Gloss/toner refreshes are offered 4–6 weeks after a highlight.
  • Formal updos for events: come with clean, dry hair and a button-down shirt.
  • Haircut-only appointments are for current clients (guests over 15).
