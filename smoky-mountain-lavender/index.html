How it's grown

Patient rows, tended by hand.

Lavender takes its time. Here is how a plant gets from a late-winter furrow to your kitchen table.

Organic practices (not certified) Integrated pest management No pesticides, herbicides or fertilizer
  1. 1

    Planted in late winter

    New plants go into the ground by hand in late winter.

  2. 2

    About two years to first harvest

    Each plant grows for roughly two years before its first cut, then stays productive for about 3 to 8 years.

  3. 3

    Replanted every year

    New rows go in each season so the field keeps blooming.

  4. 4

    Harvested by hand

    Every flower is cut by hand. No pesticides, herbicides or fertilizer.

  5. 5

    Distilled on a winter weekend

    Lavender is steam-distilled into essential oil on the farm.
