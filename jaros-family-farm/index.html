How they're raised
They never see the same ground twice.
Jaros Family Farm, on Appalachian Grown

Our Berkshire hogs spend their lives in the woods around us, free to forage and uproot across a very large area. Every animal is rotated in electric fencing to keep it safe and give it plenty of fresh forage.
