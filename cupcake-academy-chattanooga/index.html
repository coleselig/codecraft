Ordering 101

The syllabus, in plain sight.

Every rule you need before you order, collected in one place.

1

Plan about 2 weeks ahead

Typical lead time is 2 weeks. Rush orders (7 days or less) carry a $75 fee.

2

$30 order minimum

A dozen cupcakes gets you there.

3

50% deposit within 24 hours

Deposits are non-refundable and non-transferable. The balance is due 5 days before pickup.

4

Pickup, or delivery for $20

All orders are picked up unless you add delivery. Wedding delivery and setup is $25.

5

Be on time for pickup

15-minute grace period, then $20 per 15 minutes.

6

Weddings start with a consult

Fill out the consult survey; expect a follow-up within 24–48 hours.
