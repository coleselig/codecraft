Free website check-up for Volunteer Landscaping

We looked at volunteerlandscapi.wixsite.com/website, its Book Online and Services pages, on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1“Book Now” buttons don't lead to a booking

Saw On the home page, Seasonal, Lawn Care, Gardening and Clean-Ups each have a Book Now button, and those service entries link to #. The Book Online page lists six services, and each of them also links to #.

Cost Someone ready to hire you taps “Book Now” and nothing happens, so they have to go looking for your number instead.

Fix Point the buttons at a working booking page, or change them to “Call for a free estimate” with your number.

2Booking page shows template prices and a duplicate

Saw On the Book Online page, Seasonal, Lawn Care and Gardening are each listed as 1 hr for $19.99, and there is a service called Copy of Mowing, Edging, Blowing, Etc. next to the original.

Cost $19.99 is the builder's default price, so customers may expect a price you don't actually charge, and a “Copy of” entry looks unfinished.

Fix Set your real prices (or “Free estimate”) and delete the copied entry.

3Services have no descriptions on the home page

Saw Under Seasonal, Lawn Care, Gardening and Clean-Ups there is only a Book Now button, with no text about what each one includes.

Cost A visitor can't tell what “Seasonal” or “Clean-Ups” covers, so they're less likely to reach out.

Fix Add one or two lines under each, like the list on your Book Online page (Mowing, Edging, Blowing, Etc.).

4A Wix ad sits at the top of the site

Saw The home and Services pages show This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button that links to Wix.

Cost The first button a visitor sees sends them to Wix's sign-up page instead of to you.

Fix Move to a site without the ad banner and use that spot for your phone number.

5Footer still says ©2022

Saw ©2022 by Volunteer Landscaping. Proudly created with Wix.com

Cost An old date can make people wonder whether you're still taking new customers.

Fix Show the current year, and replace the Wix line with your phone number and service area.

What's working

You have a memorable tagline (Ready! Set! Mow!), a warm About section (It's no secret that we love what we do.), your location (Maryville, Tennessee), your phone (865) 320-4598 and email on the home page, plus a contact form and a link to your Facebook page. That's a solid base to build on.
