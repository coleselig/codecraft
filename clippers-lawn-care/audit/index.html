Free website check-up for Clipper's Lawn Care

We looked at clipperslawncare42.wixsite.com/website and its Book Online page on Nov 17, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1The booking page shows template services

Saw The Book Online page lists three identical items: Service Name · 1 hr · $19.99 · Book Now.

Cost A customer can't tell what they would be booking or paying for, so they're likely to leave instead of booking.

Fix Replace these with your real services (Standard Cut, Hedge/Bush Trimming, Pressure Washing), or take the page down and send people to "Call for a free estimate".

2Eight placeholder videos on the home page

Saw Eight video entries titled Video Title by Publisher Name, each with the description This is a great space to update your audience with a description of your video.

Cost Leftover template text makes the business look unfinished, and that can make a visitor doubt the work itself.

Fix Swap in before/after photos of real yards you've cut, or remove the section.

3A Wix ad appears on both pages we checked

Saw This site was designed with the .com website builder. Create your website today. with a Start Now button that links to Wix.

Cost The first button a visitor sees sends them to someone else's sign-up page instead of to you.

Fix Move to a site without the ad banner and put "Call for a free estimate" in that spot.

4Footer still says ©2020

Saw ©2020 by Clipper's Lawn Care. Proudly created with Wix.com

Cost A six-year-old date can make people wonder whether you're still in business.

Fix Show the current year, and replace the Wix line with your phone number and service area.

5Social icons point to Wix, not to you

Saw Next to your own Facebook link, the site shows Wix's icons: facebook.com/wix, twitter.com/Wix, linkedin.com/company/wix-com.

Cost People who tap those icons to see your work end up on Wix's pages, not yours.

Fix Remove the Wix icons and keep only facebook.com/ClippersLawnCare423.

6The menu is just "Home, Book Online, More"

Saw The navigation shows Home, Book Online and More, and More has no link of its own.

Cost On a phone, a visitor has to scroll to find your number instead of tapping a clear "Call" or "Contact" button.

Fix Use a short menu (Services, Photos, Contact) and add a tap-to-call button that stays on screen.

What's working

Your service area (Chattanooga, TN / NW Georgia), your experience (over 20 years of Lawn Care experience) and a clear offer (Standard Cut, Edge, Trim, and Cleanup service offered weekly or Bi-weekly) are already on the home page, and your phone and email are listed. That's a solid base to build on.
