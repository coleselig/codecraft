Free website check-up

Precision Automotive

We read through your website on November 18, 2026, page by page, the way a Morristown driver with a check-engine light would. Your phone, address and experience come through clearly. Here are a few small things that would help the site look as sharp as the work you do.

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1The Facebook icon goes to Wix, not to you

What we saw

The Facebook icon on the pages we checked links to facebook.com/wix, the website builder’s own page.

Why it matters

A customer who taps it to see your reviews or photos ends up on a software company’s page instead of yours.

Easy fix

Point the icon at your shop’s Facebook page, or remove it if you don’t use one.

2Template placeholder text in the slideshow

What we saw

The image cards near the top of the home page still read I’m a title and I’m a description. Click to edit me (three times).

Why it matters

It’s the first thing on the page, and it tells visitors the site was never finished, which can make them wonder about attention to detail.

Easy fix

Replace each card with a real service (brakes, diagnostics, maintenance) and one line about it, or remove the slideshow.

3Your years in business don’t add up anymore

What we saw

The home page says Fourteen years in business and also Founded in 2009.

Why it matters

2009 was seventeen years ago, so the page undersells you and makes it look like the text hasn’t been touched in a while.

Easy fix

Use “Serving Morristown since 2009”, which never goes out of date.

4A few typos on key pages

What we saw

The Repairs page says We offer many different servies to our customer’s. and the best parts avaiable with a 12 month 12,000 mile warrenty. The home page address reads Morristow,Tn 37814.

Why it matters

Your warranty is a real selling point, and small spelling slips right next to it are the kind of thing careful customers notice.

Easy fix

Quick edit: services, customers, available, warranty, Morristown, TN.

5A Wix ad and a generic page title

What we saw

The top of each page shows a Wix ad (This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. on the home page). The home page title, the text shown on the browser tab and in search results, is HOME | precisionauto, and we found no page description.

Why it matters

The first button on your site advertises another company, and a title of “HOME” tells someone searching for a mechanic nothing about what you do or where.

Easy fix

Change the title to something like “Auto Repair in Morristown, TN | Precision Automotive” and add a one-line description in Wix’s SEO settings. A paid plan removes the ad.

6Hours only appear on the home page

What we saw

The home page lists MONDAY - FRIDAY: 8:00 AM -6:00 PM, but the Contact and Location pages, where people go to plan a visit, show the address and phone without hours.

Why it matters

Someone deciding when to drop off a car goes straight to Contact or Location, and has to go back to the home page to find out if you’re open.

Easy fix

Add your hours (and the night drop) to the Contact and Location pages.

What's working

  • Real expertise, clearly stated. James Kleiner being an ASE Master Technician with 25 years in automotive repair is front and center, which is exactly what builds trust.
  • A strong warranty. A 12 month / 12,000 mile warranty on parts, plus towing and a night drop, are great reasons to choose you.
  • Easy to reach. Your phone (423-254-1444), fax, email and address on West Andrew Johnson Hwy are listed on the Contact page.
