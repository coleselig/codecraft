Ombre Nails

We read through ombrenailstn.com on November 17, 2026, page by page, the way a new customer looking for a nail salon would. Your hours, prices and contact info are all there, which is great. Here are a few small things that would help the site look as polished as your nails.

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site. Most are quick fixes.

1Template placeholder text is showing in the home page gallery

What we saw

In the gallery section of the home page, the five photo captions read Badget Text, which is leftover text from the website template.

Why it matters

Placeholder text makes a site look unfinished, and it sits right in the gallery, the section people look at to judge your work.

Quick fix

Replace each caption with the design name (e.g. “Chrome French tips”) or simply remove the captions.

2The Booking page doesn't actually let people book

What we saw

The BOOK NOW button goes to a page titled Nail Salons Booking Online at Ombre Nails, but there is no booking calendar or form there, just a Book now! line that isn't linked, plus your phone, email and hours.

Why it matters

A visitor who taps “Book now” expects to pick a time. When they can't, some will call, but others will book at a salon that offers online booking.

Quick fix

Add a free online booking tool (Square Appointments, Fresha, Booksy and others work well for salons) and link the button to it. Or, until then, relabel the button “Call to book” so it matches what happens.

3A few spelling mistakes on the Services menu

What we saw

On the Services page we noticed looking prety, Makeing your nails longer, supper fine extensions, fine, week, or sparse lashes, and Check under Threading (likely “Chest”). The home page About section says we believes.

Why it matters

Customers read the menu closely before choosing a service, so small typos there are noticed more than anywhere else.

Quick fix

Quick edit pass: prety → pretty, Makeing → Making, supper → super, week → weak, Check → Chest, we believes → we believe.

4A broken link in the “about nail salon” section

What we saw

Near the bottom of the home page, the link nail salon open Sunday points to a malformed address (/nail%20salon near me 37919) instead of a real page.

Why it matters

Anyone who taps it lands on an error page. That section also reads like a list of search keywords, which feels a little robotic to real customers.

Quick fix

Point that link to your Contact page (where the Sunday hours are) and rewrite the paragraph in a normal, friendly voice.

5The footer still says 2024

What we saw

Every page we checked ends with Copyright © 2024 Ombre Nails.

Why it matters

An old year can make people wonder whether prices and hours on the site are still current.

Quick fix

Update it to 2026, or set it to update automatically each year.

What's working

  • Your prices are posted. The Services page lists clear prices (e.g. Basic Manicure $27), which many salon sites skip.
  • Contact info is everywhere. Your home page has your address, tap-to-call phone, email, a map, and hours (Mon–Sat 9:30–7:30, Sun 12–6).
  • Sanitation is spelled out. The “Grade-A Sanitation” section explains sterilized tools and single-use items, which really reassures new customers.
