How booking works

Four steps to a reserved date.

The fine print, made simple, so you know exactly what to expect before you book.

  1. Plan ahead

    Book 72+ hours out

    Sessions must be booked at least 72 hours before the planned session date.

  2. Hold your date

    Pay a 40% retainer

    Due at booking and non-refundable, but it’s applied to your final price. Your date isn’t reserved until it’s paid. Specials are paid in full.

  3. Paperwork

    Sign the contract

    The contract/release form is signed at booking, if it wasn’t done before.

  4. Talk it through

    Consult session

    Every session includes a consult to go over the plan, by phone or Skype or in person.
