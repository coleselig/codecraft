Free website check-up

Asian Market, Kingsport

We read through your website on November 18, 2026, the way someone in Kingsport looking for Asian groceries would. Your hours, phone number and address are all there, which is the most important part. Here are a few small things that would help the site feel as fresh as your produce.

Checked November 18, 20265 things to tidy upNo cost, no strings

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1A typo in the line about your fresh produce

What we saw

Under FRESH VEGETABLES AND SEAFOOD on the home page: We go to Atlanta Georgia every week to get the frestest produce that we can provide. A little further down, the frozen-foods section starts with a lowercase we have hundreds of different exotic frozen and refrigerated goods.

Why it matters

The weekly trip to Atlanta is a great reason to shop with you, and it is the sentence people are most likely to read. A typo right there is the kind of thing customers notice.

Easy fix

“frestest” → “freshest”, and capitalize the “We” at the start of the frozen-foods sentence. Two-minute edit.

2The footer still says 2017 and advertises Wix

What we saw

The bottom of the page reads ©2017 BY ASIAN MARKET. PROUDLY CREATED WITH WIX.COM, and the top of the page shows a Wix banner: This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button.

Why it matters

An old year can make visitors wonder whether the hours and details are still current, and the first button on your site advertises another company instead of your store.

Easy fix

Change the footer to “© 2026 Asian Market, Kingsport TN” and remove the “created with Wix” line. The top banner goes away on a paid Wix plan, or by moving to a site that includes your own address.

3Your web address is “/website”

What we saw

The site lives at linlinda16888.wixsite.com/website. The page title shown in the browser tab and in search results is Home | Asian Market, without Kingsport, and the page has no meta description (the short summary search engines can show under the title).

Why it matters

That address is hard to say out loud, print on a bag or remember. And someone searching for an Asian grocery in Kingsport gets no hint from the title about where you are.

Easy fix

Use a short domain such as your store name dot com, change the title to something like “Asian Market | Asian Grocery on Fort Henry Dr, Kingsport TN”, and add a one-sentence description in Wix’s SEO settings.

4Photos are labeled with camera file names

What we saw

Most images on the home page are described only by their file names, for example Image (2).jpeg and WIN_20170129_12_17_47_Pro.jpg. Only the first photo has a real description (Shopping Basket).

Why it matters

Those labels are what screen readers say out loud and what search engines read to understand a photo. A file name tells them nothing about your produce, seafood or shelves.

Easy fix

Give each photo a short plain description, like “Fresh bok choy and vegetables at Asian Market Kingsport”. If any photos are from 2017, a few new phone photos of the store today would help too.

5Hours are written two different ways

What we saw

In the hours list, most days read like Mon: 9am - 7pm, but Friday reads Fri: 10:00 - 7pm.

Why it matters

It’s small, but hours are the main thing people check before driving over, and a mixed format makes Friday easy to misread.

Easy fix

Write Friday as “10am – 7pm” to match the other days.

What's working

  • Everything a shopper needs is on one page. Your full weekly hours (including that you’re closed Thursdays), phone number (423) 765-1826, and the address at 1711 Fort Henry Dr are all listed.
  • A clear reason to visit. The weekly trip to Atlanta for fresh vegetables and seafood, and goods from China, Japan, Korea, Vietnam and the Philippines, tell people exactly why to shop with you.
  • Easy to follow and find. Your Facebook page and Google Maps location are linked, and the About section’s note that you’ve been open since October 2012 and “are still growing and improving” is warm and genuine.
