From the field

What grows at Southern Oak

Naturally grown, organic practices (not certified), low spray. Here is what the farm offers.

Fresh flowers

Cut them yourself in the field. Southern Oak is one of the few local farms open to the public all summer for U-Cut flowers.

Dried flowers

Lavender and flowers hung and dried for bundles, wreaths and the pantry shelf.

Edible flowers

Blooms grown for the plate as well as the vase.

Salves

Handmade skin care, sold from the farm store during flower season.

Soap

Handmade bars to take a little of the field home with you.

Blackberries

Fruit trees and berries were planted in the fall of 2022.

Why Southern Oak

A farm you can walk into, not just buy from.

Visitors of all ages are invited to explore the lavender and flowers. Come for an afternoon in the rows, a guided tour, or a celebration under the pavilion.

  • Open all summer for U-CutOne of the few local farms that opens to the public for U-Cut flowers through the summer.
  • Naturally grownOrganic practices (not certified) and low spray.
  • Woman-owned, new farmA woman-owned New and Beginning Farmer, operating ten years or less.
  • Events, tours & field tripsAn outdoor pavilion for weddings, parties and festivals, plus guided farm tours and school field trips.
