Free website check-up for Chilhowee Lawn Care & Pressure Washing

We looked at bettiscory.wixsite.com/chilhoweelawncareand and its Book Online page on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1The Seasonal section repeats the House Washing text

Saw Both HOUSE WASHING and SEASONAL use the same subheading, Meticulous and Comprehensive, and the same three sentences, starting Keep your property in tip-top shape with our Seasonal services.

Cost A homeowner looking for pressure washing can't tell what your house washing actually includes, or what "Seasonal" means.

Fix Give each its own short description: what house washing covers (siding, brick, gutters?) and what seasonal work you do (leaf cleanup, mulch?).

2The booking page has nothing to book

Saw Book Online shows Nothing to book right now. Check back soon.

Cost Someone ready to hire you taps Book Online from the menu and hits a dead end.

Fix Replace it with a simple "Request a free estimate" form, or point the menu to your phone number.

3Some buttons don't do anything

Saw The Book Now under House Washing, the Learn More button at the top, and the Contact button under Family Business aren't linked to anything.

Cost Visitors tap them and nothing happens, which can feel like the site is broken.

Fix Link every button to a call or estimate request.

4Social icons go to Wix, not to you

Saw The footer icons link to facebook.com/wix, twitter.com/Wix and linkedin.com/company/wix-com.

Cost One of your reviews says they found you on Facebook, but people tapping the icon land on Wix's page instead of yours.

Fix Link the Facebook icon to your own page and remove the others.

5Wix ad on top, Wix credit in the footer

Saw This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button at the top, and ©2024 by Chilhowee lawn care and pressure washing. Proudly created with Wix.com at the bottom.

Cost The first button on the page sends visitors to Wix's sign-up page instead of to your phone.

Fix Move to a site without the banner, put "Call for a free estimate" in that spot, and update the footer year.

What's working

Your service area is spelled out (Cleveland, Benton, Etowah, Englewood and Ooltewah, Tn), your phone and email are listed with a working Contact Us call button, and you have four real customer reviews on the page. That's a solid base to build on.
