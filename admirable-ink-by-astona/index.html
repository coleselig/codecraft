Before you book

Good to know,
in plain English.

Her studio policies, laid out so there are no surprises on the day.

No deposit = no appointment.
Deposits are non-refundable and go toward your tattoo. They secure your slot and cover drawing time.
01

One reschedule

You get one reschedule. After that, a new deposit is needed.

02

15-minute grace

Running late? There’s a 15-minute grace period, then the appointment is rescheduled or cancelled.

03

72-hour notice

No-shows and cancellations within 72 hours carry a 20% fee.

04

One guest, no kids

Space is limited, so you can bring one guest. Children aren’t permitted.

05

Eat & rest first

“Please make sure that you eat and get plenty of rest” before your appointment.

06

Love it before you leave

Make sure you’re happy with your tattoo before you leave the chair.
