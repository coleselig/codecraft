From the field

What comes out of the dirt

Organic practices (not certified), low spray and GMO-free, alongside conventional growing. Here is what the farm lists.

Fruit & berries

Strawberries, blueberries, blackberries, peaches, apples, muscadines, cantaloupes, melons and watermelons.

Greens

Kale, collards, Swiss chard, mustard greens, arugula, Asian greens, lettuce, salad greens and salad mix.

Roots & alliums

Carrots, beets, potatoes, sweet potatoes, parsnips, radish, daikon, turnips, garlic, onions, green onions and shallots.

Summer vegetables

Tomatoes, sweet corn, okra, cucumber, eggplant, summer squash, sweet and hot peppers, chiles, green beans and pole beans.

Cool-season crops

Broccoli, Brussels sprouts, cabbage, kohlrabi and peas, plus dry beans for the pantry.

Fall & flowers

Pumpkins, winter squash and gourds, fresh herbs and fresh flowers.

Where to find us

At the farm, at market, at your door

Market Square Farmers Market

Downtown Knoxville. Dirt Poor Farm is listed among the market's farms.

2026 season: May 2 – Oct 31

More farmers markets

Dixie Lee Farmers Market, Historic Oak Ridge Farmers Market and the Chattanooga Farmers Market (Sundays).

The farm in Sweetwater

Onsite farm store, CSA pickup and farm tours by appointment. Farm pickup orders need 24 hours' notice. Credit/debit, EBT/SNAP and SNAP incentives accepted.

Delivered by Market Wagon

Thursday delivery (order by Tuesday) across Knox, Blount, Loudon, McMinn, Hamilton, Bradley and more East Tennessee counties.
