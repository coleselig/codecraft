How we farm

Raised on pasture. Grown with care.

These are the practices the farm lists for itself.

Pasture raised & free range

Chickens range freely and the cattle and hogs are raised on pasture, humanely.

Permaculture

The farm is planned around permaculture, working with the land rather than against it.

Organic methods

Grown with organic, sustainable methods. Not certified organic.

Woman-owned & LGBTQ+

A woman-owned, LGBTQ+ farm run by a new and beginning farmer.
