Free website check-up for Anything Automotive & Towing

We looked at anything-automotive.wixsite.com/towing and its Towing page on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1The Jump Starts card describes lock outs

Saw On the Towing page, the Jump Starts card says We offer $80.00 Lock outs., the same sentence as the Lock Outs card.

Cost Someone with a dead battery reads about lock outs instead, and may not be sure you actually do jump starts.

Fix Change the card text to describe jump starts.

2No hours anywhere

Saw Neither the home page nor the Towing page says when you're available: no hours, and no "24/7" or "after hours" line.

Cost When someone is stuck on the side of the road at night, they need to know right away whether calling you is worth it.

Fix Put your hours (or "24/7") next to the phone number at the top of every page.

3The footer Facebook icon goes to Wix

Saw Your header Facebook icon correctly goes to facebook.com/anything.automtive/, but the one in the footer goes to facebook.com/wix, and the footer Yelp icon goes to yelp.com instead of your Yelp page.

Cost People who scroll down and tap those icons to check your reviews land on the wrong pages.

Fix Point the footer icons at the same Facebook and Yelp pages as the header.

4A Wix ad is the first thing at the top

Saw This site was designed with the .com website builder. Create your website today. with a Start Now button that links to Wix.

Cost The first button a visitor sees sends them to someone else's sign-up page instead of to you.

Fix Move to a site without the ad banner and put a big tap-to-call button for (865) 607-0378 in that spot.

5The footer credits someone else, with a typo

Saw © 2023 by Business Solutios. Proudly created with Wix.com

Cost A three-year-old date and an unfamiliar, misspelled company name can make people wonder whether the site is still yours and still current.

Fix Show the current year and your own business name, or your phone number and service area instead.

What's working

Your phone ((865) 607-0378) and email are on the page, along with the service area (Serving Knoxville and Surrounding Areas), Paul's 15+ years of towing experience, clear prices on the Towing page, real customer testimonials, and correct links to your own Facebook, Yelp and Twitter in the header. That's a solid base to build on.
