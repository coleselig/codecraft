Chattanooga's mobile coffee truck

Good coffee, parked where you are.

Spill the Beans started as a weekend trailer by Coolidge Park. Today it's a full-time coffee truck serving Chattanooga markets, ballgames and races, with beans roasted locally by Chattanooga Roasting Company.

Why book Spill the Beans

Chattanooga coffee, roasted by the founder

Lisa Dunny now runs Chattanooga Roasting Company full-time, and she still roasts Spill the Beans' coffee. You get the same cup that built the truck, poured by Danielle and Josh Phillips.

  • A regular at the Chattanooga Market. The truck is featured in the Market's 25th Season vendor profile series.
  • Big-crowd ready. They've served events around town, including Finley Stadium and the Head of the Hooch regatta each November.
  • Fully mobile. A truck since 2015, so it comes to your lot, field or venue.
