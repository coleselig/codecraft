Before you book

Good to know.

Hair is provided

Pre-stretched hair is included for braiding services. Colors 1, 1B, 2, 4, 27, 30, 613 and Red; note your color in the booking message.

Come washed & blow-dried

Many styles need freshly washed, blow-dried hair. If it needs a wash at the appointment, a $25 wash fee applies.

Travel available

Kayla can travel to you for an extra $25. Payment by cash, Cash App, Apple Pay or PayPal.
