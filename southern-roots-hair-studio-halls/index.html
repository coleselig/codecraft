About

Rooted in Halls

Southern Roots Hair Studio is Amanda Wood's chair on Maynardville Pike in the Halls area of Knoxville. The menu centers on color: full and partial highlight retouches and full color, along with shampoo, cut and style, and eyebrow waxing.

Every service can be booked online, so you can see open times and choose what fits. You can also find the studio on Facebook as Southern Roots Hair Studio.
