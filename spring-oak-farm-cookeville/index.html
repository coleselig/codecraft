How we grow

A family farm that picks varieties by flavor.

The work on Spring Oak Farm is done by the family itself, in the Rickman community north of Cookeville.

6

Greenhouses

Mostly hydroponic growing under cover stretches the season, with some crops coming year-round, plus field crops when the weather allows.

0

Insecticides or fungicides

The family uses companion crops to help with insect control instead of spraying.

3

Generations

A three-generation farm where family members supply the labor, from seed starting to market day.
