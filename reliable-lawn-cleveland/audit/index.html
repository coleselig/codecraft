Free website check-up

Reliable Lawn Services

We read through your website on November 18, 2026, the way a Cleveland homeowner looking for a mowing crew would. Your phone number, email and a real customer review are right there, which is great. Here are a few small things that would make the site work as hard as you do.

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1A raw Yardbook web address is printed as text

What we saw

Right after the Get a Free Quote button on the home page, the link text is the full address itself: https://www.yardbook.com/new_quote/adbcb81c7f76fd1655be279b88d74a78ea7904be

Why it matters

A long string of letters and numbers looks like a mistake to visitors and doesn’t tell them what happens if they tap it, so most won’t.

Easy fix

Turn it into a button with plain words, like “Request a quote online”, that opens the same Yardbook form.

2“Book Now” opens an email instead of booking

What we saw

The Book Now and Get a Free Quote buttons on the home page open a blank email to reliablelawnservicesTN@gmail.com (subject “Booking Request” or “Quote Request”). Your Book Online page lists Lawn Mowing, Hedge Trimming and Mulch Installation, each marked Price Varies.

Why it matters

On a phone, an email link often opens an app the visitor doesn’t use, and the email starts empty, so they have to guess what details you need. You already have a Yardbook quote form that asks the right questions.

Easy fix

Send “Get a Free Quote” and “Book Now” to your Yardbook form, and keep the email address as a backup in the Contact section.

3Your phone number isn’t tap-to-call

What we saw

The header reads Call Us Now: 423.464.9333, and the quote section says or call us at 423.464.9333. In the page we read, neither number is a link.

Why it matters

Most people looking for lawn care are on their phone. If the number can’t be tapped, they have to copy or memorize it, and some just call the next company instead.

Easy fix

Link the number so it dials when tapped (in Wix: select the text, add a link, choose “Phone number”).

4Services are listed with no description

What we saw

On the home page, Lawn Mowing, Hedge Trimming and Mulch Installation appear as headings only, with no text beneath them.

Why it matters

Homeowners want to know what’s included, for example whether mowing covers edging and blowing off the driveway, before they ask for a quote.

Easy fix

Add one or two sentences under each service saying what’s included and roughly how often you visit.

5A Wix ad sits at the top of the site

What we saw

The first thing on the page is the banner This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button, and the address is reliablelawnservic.wixsite.com/reliable-lawn-servic (the business name is cut off at “servic”).

Why it matters

The first button visitors see advertises another company, and a long, cut-off address is hard to print on a truck magnet or say over the phone.

Easy fix

A paid Wix plan removes the banner and lets you connect a short domain of your own, or move to a site that includes one.

What's working

  • A real customer review. Michael D.’s note, “Reliable Lawn Services transformed my unruly lawn into a stunning outdoor oasis,” is the kind of proof new customers look for.
  • Full contact details. Your address (850 Deer Run Ln NW, Cleveland), email and phone are all listed in the Contact section, and the phone is in the header too.
  • Clear search listing. The page title, “Reliable Lawn Services | Lawn mowing in cleveland | Tennessee”, says what you do and where, which is exactly right.
