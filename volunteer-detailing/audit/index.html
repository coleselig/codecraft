Volunteer Mobile Detail

We read through your Wix site on November 18, 2026, the way someone in Knoxville looking for a mobile detailer would. Your four services are explained in plain language, from a basic wash all the way to a wheels-off deep clean, so it's easy to tell what you do. Here are a few things that would help the site work as hard as you do.

Checked November 18, 20266 things to tidy upNo cost, no strings

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1Booking is a “copy this link” instead of a button

What we saw

The first heading on the page says Copy This Link To Book Through Square!, followed by the raw address https://volunteermobiledetail.square.site as a heading. There's no “Book Now” button anywhere else on the page.

Why it matters

Booking is the most important thing a visitor can do. Asking them to copy a web address feels like extra work, especially on a phone, and some people will stop there.

Quick fix

Replace the heading and address with a large “Book Now” button that links to your Square page, and add the same button to the menu and next to each service.

2“Which fits your needs?” has nothing to compare

What we saw

Under WHICH FITS YOUR NEEDS? there's one sentence: Hire us for any of these services and learn how we cater to the needs of each client. No prices, starting prices or hours appear anywhere on the site.

Why it matters

The heading promises a choice, but there's nothing to choose from. People shopping for a detail usually want a rough idea of cost and availability before they reach out.

Quick fix

Add a short list of “starting at” prices for each service (or a note that prices depend on vehicle size), plus the days you usually work.

3The business goes by four different names

What we saw

The browser tab says Volunteer Auto Mobile Detail. On the page we saw ABOUT VOLUNTEER DETAILING, WELCOME TO VOLUNTEER MOBILE DETAIL and Our mission at Volunteer Detail is simple. The footer says ©2023 by Volunteer Detailing. Your Square page is Volunteer Mobile Detail.

Why it matters

When someone searches for you later or tells a friend, one consistent name is much easier to find and remember.

Quick fix

Pick one name (Volunteer Mobile Detail matches your Square page) and use it in the headings, the footer and the page title in Wix's SEO settings.

4A Wix ad banner sits at the top of the page

What we saw

The first thing on the page is This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button. The address is simpsonwalker.wixsite.com/volunteerdetailing.

Why it matters

The banner pushes your business down and invites visitors to click away. A long wixsite address with a personal name in it is also hard to put on a truck magnet or say out loud.

Quick fix

Connect a short domain of your own and remove the free-plan banner.

5Your phone number at the top can't be tapped

What we saw

865-466-7338 appears at the top of the page and in GET IN TOUCH as plain text. Only a small Contact button is set up to call.

Why it matters

Most people looking for a mobile detailer are on their phone. When they see your number, they expect to tap it and call or text right away.

Quick fix

Highlight each phone number in Wix, click the link icon and choose “Phone” so every one of them taps to call.

6An old year and a small typo

What we saw

The footer still says ©2023. The Ceramic Coatings text reads Creating A Extra Layer Of Protection.

Why it matters

An old year can make people wonder if the business is still active, and small slips are what people notice when they're comparing a few detailers.

Quick fix

Update the footer to 2026 and change it to “Creating An Extra Layer Of Protection.”

What's working

  • Your services are easy to understand. Car Detailing, Ceramic Coatings, Interior Detail and Deep Clean each say what's included, like Full Wheel Removal and Great For Removing Salt/Mud & Preventing Rust.
  • Your contact form fits a mobile business. It asks for an address, so you know where the car is before you reply.
  • Online booking is already set up. The Square link on the page works, so customers can book without a phone call.
