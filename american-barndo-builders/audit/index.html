Free website check-up for American Barndo Builders

We looked at usbarnbuilders1.wixsite.com/americanbarnbuilders on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1A testimonial has two different names on it

Saw The review is headed Riley Jones but signed -Carlos Ribertson, Chapel Hill-. Both look like names left over from the website template.

Cost For a home builder, reviews carry a lot of weight. A mismatched name can make a careful buyer doubt the review, and then the rest of the page.

Fix Replace it with a real quote from one of your customers (first name and town is plenty), or remove the section until you have one.

2Social icons point to Wix, not to you

Saw The Twitter, LinkedIn and Facebook icons in the footer go to twitter.com/Wix, linkedin.com/company/wix-com and facebook.com/wix.

Cost People who tap them hoping to see more of your builds end up on Wix's pages.

Fix Link your own pages if you have them, or remove the icons.

3A Wix ad is the first thing at the top

Saw This site was designed with the .com website builder. Create your website today. with a Start Now button that links to Wix.

Cost The first button a visitor sees sends them to someone else's sign-up page instead of to you.

Fix Move to a site without the ad banner and put "Call for a free quote" with your number in that spot.

4The menu repeats itself

Saw The menu lists Home, About, Services, Projects, Contact, Testimonials, Projects, About, FAQ, Projects, More. That's Projects three times and About twice, and each one goes to a different page.

Cost A visitor can't tell which "Projects" page has your best work, and may give up before they find it.

Fix Keep one of each (Home, Services, Projects, About, FAQ, Contact) and merge the duplicate pages.

5Project cards show only a title and a date

Saw Some of Our Latest Work lists Custom stick built Barndos, Barndominium and Contemporary Home, each with only a date such as January 25, 2020 and no description underneath.

Cost Someone pricing a barndo wants to see size, layout and finish. A bare title doesn't show off what you can build.

Fix Add a photo set and a line or two per project (size, location, features like the 2x6 framing and 40-year metal).

6A typo in your list of states

Saw We are currently building in Alabama Arkansas North Carolina South Carolina Georgia Indiana Kentucky Tennesse Mississippi, with Tennesse missing its last "e" and no commas between the states.

Cost It's small, but it's your home state, and the run-on list is hard to scan on a phone.

Fix Spell it "Tennessee" and show the states as a short list or a map.

What's working

The site already has your phone (1-931-287-4602), email and Crossville Tennessee, Cumberland County address, a contact form, a clear list of what you build (pole barns, garages, workshops, barndominiums, shouses), your build specs, a discount list for veterans and first responders, and a helpful local-codes notice. That's a solid base to build on.
