From pasture to package

Raised, made and wrapped in Bulls Gap.

Jimmy and Minnie Sentelle raise purebred Berkshire hogs, plus some Landrace-Berkshire crosses, out on pasture. Since 2010 the sausage has been made in their own inspected facility on the farm.

Berkshire on pasture

Purebred Berkshire hogs and some Landrace-Berkshire crosses, raised outdoors on the farm.

Nothing extra

No preservatives and no MSG. Just pork and seasoning, the way the family makes it.
