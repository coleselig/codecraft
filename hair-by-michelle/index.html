The approach

One stylist. Your time.

No assembly line: just Michelle, her chair, and as long as your hair actually needs.

i.

Color made for you

The salon is built around customized hair coloring and treatment options, from glazes to full foils.

ii.

Time, not a timer

I will take my time to provide the best service I am able to provide. A balayage is booked for three full hours.

iii.

Talk it through first

Communication is the key!! Tell her what you want, what you don’t, and what your hair has been through.

iv.

The whole family

Women’s, men’s and kids’ cuts, plus brow and face waxing, all in one Fountain City chair.
