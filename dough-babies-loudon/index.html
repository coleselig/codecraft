How it works

Order Monday, eat Friday

Order by Monday midnight

Place your order on Bakesy before the Monday-at-midnight cutoff for the next scheduled pickup. $10 minimum, about 4 days' lead time.

Pay your way

Venmo or cash at pickup, or text Kim for a Square link to pay by card (small fee).

Pick up fresh

Fresh Bread Friday, 9:00–11:00 AM in Loudon. Local pickup only, no shipping.
