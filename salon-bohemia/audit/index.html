Salon Bohemia

We read through your Wix site on November 18, 2026, the way someone looking for a new colorist would. Your three color packages are laid out clearly with prices, and the list of everything included in every color appointment (consultation, neck and shoulder massage, scalp massage, treatment, hand massage, blow-dry) really sells the experience. Here are a few things that would help the site work as hard as you do.

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1The name “Salon Bohemia” doesn't appear on the page

What we saw

The page is branded Naturally, Jess, and the browser tab reads Hairdresser | Naturally, Jess | Farragut. We couldn't find “Salon Bohemia” anywhere in the visible text, even though your Instagram and Facebook links (salon.bohemia.knox, salonbohemiaknox) use that name.

Why it matters

Someone who hears about Salon Bohemia or finds you on Instagram may land here and not be sure they're in the right place. It also makes it harder for the site to show up when people search the salon's name.

Quick fix

Add “Salon Bohemia” near the top (for example “Salon Bohemia · Naturally, Jess”) and put it in the page title under Wix's SEO settings.

2Typos in the packages section, right where people decide

What we saw

The middle package is named REJUVINATE, the Refresh package is described as a maintenence package, and the intro says I provide a custom, one on one experince.

Why it matters

This is the part of the page people read most carefully before booking a $100–$160 service. Spelling slips here stand out more than anywhere else.

Quick fix

Change them to “REJUVENATE,” “maintenance package” and “one-on-one experience.”

3A Wix ad banner sits at the top of the page

What we saw

The page opens with a Wix banner saying the site was designed with the Wix.com website builder, followed by Create your website today. and a Start Now button. The address is jessicamccroskey.wixsite.com/website-1.

Why it matters

The banner is the first thing visitors see, before your name, and it invites them to click away. An address ending in website-1 is hard to remember and doesn't say “Salon Bohemia.”

Quick fix

Connect your own domain (something like your salon's name) and remove the free-plan banner.

4No hours anywhere on the page

What we saw

We couldn't find business hours, or a note that you work by appointment, anywhere on the page.

Why it matters

People looking for hours and finding none may not know whether to call, book online or wait. One line answers the question.

Quick fix

Add your hours, or simply “By appointment only. See open times in online booking.”

5The phone number is hard to read

What we saw

Your number is written as 8652087010, with no spaces or dashes, in both the header and the contact section, and it isn't set up as a call link.

Why it matters

A run of ten digits is easy to misread or mistype. Most visitors are on a phone and expect to tap a number to call.

Quick fix

Write it as (865) 208-7010 and, in the Wix editor, link it to tel:+18652087010 so it calls in one tap.

6A few more small typos

What we saw

The new-client section says Clink the link below to request a consultation! and so we cant get to know one another. Elsewhere: Your hairs health is my ultimate goal and some of the best of Knoxvilles beauty industry.

Why it matters

None of these change the message, but small slips are what people notice when they're choosing between a few salons.

Quick fix

Change them to “Click,” “can't,” “Your hair's health” and “Knoxville's,” then give the page one read-through for other missing apostrophes.

What's working

  • Clear prices. Refresh ($100), Rejuvenate ($130) and Reinvent ($160) each list what's included, plus a note that haircuts and K18 treatment are available as add-ons.
  • Booking is built in. Both book online buttons go to your Square booking page, and new clients get a request a consultation email link if they'd rather ask first.
  • Your story builds trust. The section about you as owner-master colorist-educator and your goal to create healthy, beautiful hair while providing you with a relaxing experience says exactly what makes you different.
