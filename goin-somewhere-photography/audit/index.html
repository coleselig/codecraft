Free website check-up for Goin' Somewhere Photography & Gifts

We looked at goinsomewherephoto.wixsite.com/goinsomewherephotogr on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1No list of the sessions you offer

Saw The page text says With a focus on child and family photography and mentions mini sessions and outdoor photo sessions, but there's no list of session types. Newborn, maternity, cake smash, senior, Christmas and fresh 48 only appear in the hidden image descriptions, not as text a visitor can read.

Cost Parents looking for a newborn or senior photographer can't tell that you do those sessions, and Google can't really either.

Fix Add a short “Sessions” section with one line on each type (newborn, maternity, cake smash, senior, mini sessions) and point the Services menu item to it.

2The gifts side of the business isn't shown

Saw The business name is Goin' Somewhere Photography & Gifts, but the only mention of gifts on the page is providing memories and gifts that last for years to come. No gift items are shown.

Cost Anyone who heard about your handcrafted gifts can't see what you make or how to order.

Fix Add a small gifts section with a few photos and a “Message us to order” button.

3A Wix ad sits at the top of the site

Saw The page shows This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button that links to Wix.

Cost The first button a visitor sees sends them to Wix's sign-up page instead of to you.

Fix Move to a site without the ad banner and use that spot for “Book a session”.

4Footer still says ©2021

Saw ©2021 by Goin' Somewhere Photography & Gifts. Proudly created with Wix.com

Cost A five-year-old date can make people wonder whether you're still booking sessions.

Fix Show the current year, and replace the Wix line with your phone number and service area.

What's working

You have three real reviews on the page (from Tiffany, Kathleen and Sharie, including Misty is amazing!), a clear location (We are a local small business based in Corryton, Tennessee.), a Get in Touch button that calls you directly, and a clear style: Specializing in outdoor photo sessions we utilize natural light. That's a solid base to build on.
