Touch of Serenity

We read through your Wix site on November 18, 2026, the way someone in Kingsport looking for a massage would. Helping People Get Back to Themselves is a lovely line to lead with, and the Book Now button takes people straight to your Square booking page, which is exactly what it should do. Here are a few things that would help the site work as hard as you do.

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today, compared with your Square booking page where it helps. Most are quick fixes.

1Your website and your booking page list different hours

What we saw

The website says M-F: 10am-6:30pm and Saturday: 10am-3:00pm. Your Square booking page says Monday to Friday 10:00 AM – 6:15 PM and Saturday 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM.

Why it matters

Someone who reads the website might call at 6:20 on a weekday or walk in at 10 on a Saturday and find you closed. When two pages disagree, people don't know which one to trust.

Quick fix

Decide which hours are current and update the other page so both match (your Google listing too, if it shows hours).

2No prices anywhere on the website

What we saw

The Treatments section describes nine kinds of massage but doesn't mention a single price. Your booking page already lists every service with its length and price, for example 60 min Swedish at $65 and 30 min Upper Body at $40.

Why it matters

Price is one of the first things people want to know. If they can't find it, many will move on to a spa that shows it rather than call to ask.

Quick fix

Copy the prices from your Square booking page under each treatment, or add a short price list next to the Book Now button.

3The Facebook icon points to a different page than your booking page does

What we saw

The Facebook icon on the website links to facebook.com/theknoteliminator6265. Your Square booking page links to facebook.com/touchofserenity107.

Why it matters

Visitors often check Facebook for reviews and photos before booking. Sending them to an older or different page means they may not see your current posts.

Quick fix

In the Wix editor, click the Facebook icon and change its link to the page you use now. Adding your Instagram (@touchofserenity107) next to it would help too.

4The team and treatments don't match what can be booked

What we saw

The website introduces Amber, Jill and Brittany. Brittany's section shows only her name, with no bio. It also offers Esalen Style Massage, Integrative Massage and Reiki. Your booking page lists Amber Russell, LMT and Lindsey Thompson, doesn't list those three treatments, and offers Sports Massage, Chair Massage and Red Light Therapy, which the website doesn't mention.

Why it matters

A client who reads about a therapist or treatment on the website and then can't find it when booking may give up or wonder if the site is out of date.

Quick fix

Update the team section to the people who take appointments now, and make the treatment list match your booking menu.

5A Wix ad banner sits at the top of the page

What we saw

The first thing on the page is This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. Get Started, and the footer reads Proudly created with Wix.com. The address is alrussell1326.wixsite.com/touchofserenity.

Why it matters

The banner pushes your name down and invites visitors to click away. A long wixsite address is also hard to remember or print on a business card.

Quick fix

Connect your own domain (a short name like your business name) and remove the free-plan banner.

6The footer still says 2021, plus one small typo

What we saw

The page ends with ©2021 by Touch of Serenity. Jill's bio reads I'm a licensed massage therapist with a passion for Esalen style massage and is an attuned Reiki Master.

Why it matters

An old year can make people wonder whether a business is still active, and small slips are what people notice when they're choosing between a few places.

Quick fix

Update the year to 2026 and change “and is an attuned Reiki Master” to “and am an attuned Reiki Master.”

What's working

  • Booking is one click away. The Book Now button goes straight to your Square booking page, and there's a Gift Certificates button for people buying for someone else.
  • Experience is up front. I’ve been a dedicated Licensed Massage Therapist in Tennessee since 2006 is exactly the kind of line that builds trust.
  • You're easy to find. Your full address on Commerce St., phone number and email are all on the page, and the site says you're accepting new clients.
