Free website check-up for JC Custom Detailing

We looked at jccustomdetailing.wixsite.com/jc-custom-detailing and its Services and Get a Quote pages on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1The Get a Quote page still has template text

Saw Under the Get a Quote heading: This is your Price Quote section paragraph. and Encourage your site visitors and prospective clients to provide their information in order to get a quote for your services.

Cost This is the page people visit when they're ready to book. Leftover template text there can make them wonder if the form actually reaches you.

Fix Replace it with one or two lines of your own, like what to include (vehicle, size, which services) and how fast you reply.

2A Wix ad sits at the top of every page

Saw This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button on the home page, and This site was designed with the .com website builder. Create your website today. with Start Now on the Services and Get a Quote pages.

Cost The first button a visitor sees sends them to Wix's sign-up page instead of to you.

Fix Move to a site without the ad banner and put "Get a Quote" or your phone number in that spot.

3The phone number isn't tap-to-call

Saw In the Get in Touch section, (423) 930-3998 appears as plain text, while jccustomdetailingllc@gmail.com is a clickable link.

Cost On a phone, a customer has to copy the number by hand to call you, and some won't bother.

Fix Make the number a tap-to-call link and add a "Call" button that stays on screen on phones.

4The services list ends with "etc." and has a typo

Saw The Detailing Services list reads Pressure Washing, Vacuuming, Waxing, Engine bay cleaning, Carpet Shampoo, Wheel polish, Exhaust tip polish, Headlight resoration, etc.

Cost Small slips on the services page can make careful, detail-focused work look less careful than it is.

Fix Fix the spelling to "Headlight restoration" and swap "etc." for the other things you do, like boats and homes.

5The footer still says ©2023

Saw ©2023 by JC Custom Detailing. Proudly created with Wix.com on every page we checked.

Cost An old date can make people wonder whether the site, and the business, is still active.

Fix Show the current year and replace the Wix line with your address and phone number.

What's working

Your full address (232 South Commerce Street, Johnson City, TN 37604), phone and email are on the home page with a contact form, the Services page lists eight real services and says You name it, we can do it! We service cars, boats, homes, & more!, and you already ask customers to Rate Our Service. That's a good base to build on.
