Why guests come back

Quiet, thorough, and made for you.

A real scalp analysis first

Japanese-inspired head spa sessions start with a custom look at dryness, buildup or sensitivity, using Virtue and René Furterer Paris products.

Lashes, brows and skin in one visit

Lash lifts, laminations and full sets sit on the same menu as Hydrafacial and head spa, so you can book a combo instead of a second trip.

Clear, published pricing

Every service lists its price and length before you book. No guessing, no surprise at checkout.

Something for everyone

Lumber Luxe head spas come with a beard wash, and there are tween head spa and facial options for ages 10–12.

Clinical-grade skin options

SkinPen microneedling, CO2Lift, Keravive scalp treatments and the Face Reality acne program, alongside classic facials.

Open six days a week

Early starts Wednesday, Friday and Saturday at 9am, and evenings until 7pm on Tuesday and Thursday.
