Free website check-up for Simple Services

We read through your current site page by page today. Below are the spots that could make a homeowner in Chattanooga hesitate before calling you, and how to fix each one.

Site reviewed: simpleservicesllc7.wixsite.com/simple-services

What we found

1Your FAQ page shows Wix setup instructions

What we saw
"How do I add a new question?" … "To add a new question go to app settings and press "Manage Questions" button."
FAQs page: /copy-of-file-a-claim
Why it mattersA customer with a real question about your work finds builder help text instead, which makes the business look unfinished.
The fixReplace it with 4–5 real answers: areas you serve, how estimates work, what you paint or wash, and how to reach you.

2Another company's name appears in your copy

What we saw
"At CertaPro Painters® of Chattanooga, TN we'll work with you to find a finish…"
"The CertaPro Painters® of Chattanooga, TN team can work install decorative borders…"
Residential Painting page: /copy-of-faqs (the testimonial there also reads "Based on past experience I called on CertaPro Painters®…")
Why it mattersA visitor reading about your painting work sees a competitor's name and may not be sure who they would actually be hiring.
The fixRewrite these sections in your own words, under the Simple Services name.

3Links send visitors to other businesses' websites

What we sawOn the home page, "House Painting" and "Commercial & House Painting Professionals in Chattanooga" link to ktechautorepair.com; "Exterior Painting" and "Interior Painting" link to certapro.com.
Why it mattersSomeone who taps to learn more about your painting leaves your site entirely, landing on an auto-repair shop or a competitor.
The fixPoint every service link at your own service pages.

4"Latest Projects" is still placeholder text

What we saw
"Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetuer adipiscing elit."
Under "LATEST PROJECTS" on the home and FAQs pages
Why it mattersThis is the spot where people expect to see your work, and the filler text tells them the site isn't being looked after.
The fixAdd a few before/after photos from real jobs with a one-line caption each.

5The main "Schedule an estimate" button goes nowhere new

What we sawThe home-page button "SCHEDULE AN ESTIMATE" links back to the same home page (simpleservicesllc7.wixsite.com/simple-services).
Why it mattersA visitor who is ready to book clicks the most important button on the site and nothing happens.
The fixMake it a tap-to-call link (615-801-1155) or point it to a short estimate form.

6Menu labels don't match their pages, and the site carries a Wix ad

What we saw"RESIDENTIAL PAINTING" opens /copy-of-faqs, "COMMERCIAL" opens /copy-of-residential-painting, and "FAQs" opens /copy-of-file-a-claim. The home page shows the banner
"This website was built on Wix. Create yours today."
The footer still reads "© 2021".
Why it mattersA free web address plus an ad for someone else's product make it harder for a first-time visitor to take the business seriously.
The fixRename the page addresses to match their content, move to your own domain name, and update the footer year.

What's working

Your phone (615-801-1155), email and service area ("Chattanooga and Surrounding Areas") are easy to find on the home page, and you already have a real customer review: "Very professional and quick." (Jean D, Chattanooga, TN).
