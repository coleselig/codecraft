What we grow

Tomatoes, first and almost only.

Ramirez Tomatoes grows greenhouse and field tomatoes in Rutledge. For years nearly all of it went out wholesale. Now you can buy the same tomatoes yourself.

Main crop

Greenhouse tomatoes

Our story

Our story

A family that has worked tomatoes for a lifetime, now growing them for you.

Eddie Ramirez comes from a long family history of farming. His parents spent most of their lives working tomatoes, and in 2002 they bought a farm in Rutledge. Eddie and his wife Lizbeth have strong ties to neighboring Union County. They love what they do.
