About

The studio

Reckoned Hair Studio is a hair studio in a suite on Hickory Hollow Lane in Antioch, offering frontal and traditional sew-ins, quick weaves, silk outs, sleek ponytails, loc detox and micro loc consultations, plus brow tinting and strip lashes.

Open Monday through Friday. Every service and price is listed up front, and appointments can be booked online.
