The menu

Every groom starts with a proper bath.

Shampoo matched to your pet’s skin and coat, then build up from there: a tidy-up, or the whole haircut.

Bath

The foundation
  • Shampoo for their skin & coat type
  • Blow dry and brush out
  • Eye & ear cleaning, ear hair removal
  • Nail clip
  • Sanitary trim & pad trim
  • Anal glands, if needed

  • Everything in the Bath

Deshedding

Deshedding shampoo and conditioner, plus up to 20 minutes of extra brushing.

Aloe treatment

A deep conditioner with aloe vera, jojoba and vitamins A, D & E for itchy or dry skin and coat.

Enzymatic toothpaste to cut odor-causing bacteria and help control plaque and tartar.
