The approach

The experience is the photo.

KJ puts equal focus on how the session feels and on getting the perfect frame, because relaxed people make the best pictures.

i.

Calm, fun sessions

A calm, easygoing approach that gets real, natural expressions instead of stiff poses.

ii.

Beautiful natural light

Photography that is natural, relaxed and beautifully lit, in a variety of styles.

iii.

Anywhere you choose

Every package is shot at a location of your choice: your favorite park, your home, or three spots for an elopement.

iv.

Your photos, yours to print

Every session comes with an online gallery of high-resolution images and a print release.

v.

Rooted in Knoxville since 2019

Founded in 2019 and photographing Knoxville families, couples and weddings ever since.

vi.

Personalized, not packaged

Any package can be shaped to fit your needs and your budget. Just ask.
