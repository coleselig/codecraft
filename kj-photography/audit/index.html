Free website check-up

KJ Photography

We read through your Wix site on November 18, 2026, the way a couple or a new parent shopping for a photographer would. Your prices are right there on every package page, with exactly what's included, which is more than a lot of photographers share. Here are a few small things that would help the site work as hard as you do.

Checked November 18, 20265 things to tidy upNo cost, no strings

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1Your Contact section lists a different email address

What we saw

At the top of the home page and on every package page, your email is kjphotography19@yahoo.com. But in the Contact section at the bottom of the home page, the address shown and linked is kjphotography@yahoo.com, without the 19.

Why it matters

Someone who scrolls to the bottom to get in touch could email the wrong address, and you'd never know they tried.

Quick fix

In the Wix editor, open the Contact section and change the text and the email link to kjphotography19@yahoo.com.

2Your phone number can't be tapped to call or text

What we saw

865.599.7311 shows up on the home page and on each package page we checked, but always as plain text. None of them is a call link.

Why it matters

Most people will find you on their phone. If the number doesn't tap, they have to copy it or remember it, and some won't bother.

Quick fix

Highlight the number in Wix, click the link icon and choose “Phone,” or add a small “Call or text” button that does the same.

3A Wix ad banner sits at the top of every page

What we saw

Each page opens with a Wix banner that says Create your website today. with a Start Now button, and the footer reads Proudly created with Wix.com. The address is kjphotography19.wixsite.com/website.

Why it matters

The banner is the first thing visitors see, ahead of your name and your photos, and it invites them to click away. A long wixsite address is also hard to put on a business card or say out loud.

Quick fix

Connect a short domain of your own (something like your business name) and remove the free-plan banner.

4The site still reads like it's 2019

What we saw

Every page ends with ©2019 by KJ Photography. The home page says Founded in 2019 and also that your work is brand new to Knoxville and surrounding areas!

Why it matters

An old year can make people wonder if you're still booking. And you've been shooting in Knoxville for years now, which is a strength worth saying.

Quick fix

Update the footer to 2026 and swap “brand new to Knoxville” for something like “photographing Knoxville since 2019.”

5A few typos on the home and wedding pages

What we saw

The About text says I began my photography journey at a the age of 21. On Wedding Packages, one collection is spelled Lavendar, and the engagement add-on reads Complementary (free) Engagement Session.

Why it matters

Couples comparing wedding photographers tend to read every line of the packages page, so small slips there get noticed.

Quick fix

Change them to “at the age of 21,” “Lavender” and “Complimentary (free) Engagement Session.”

What's working

  • Your prices are clear. Every package, from the $65 Standard Package to the $450 Golden wedding collection, lists the hours, number of images and what's included.
  • It's easy to reach out from any package. Each package has a Contact Me! button that opens an email to you.
  • It feels personal. Hi! I'm Kayla Johnson and your note about focusing on the overall experience help people get a feel for you before they reach out.
