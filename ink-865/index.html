04 — House rules

Good to know.

Straight from the studio's published policies. By appointment only.

Deposits hold your slot

Deposits are non-refundable. Cancelling or rescheduling forfeits it — a 2–4 hour slot is hard to fill on short notice.

Be on time

More than 30 minutes late means rescheduling and forfeiting the deposit. Documented emergencies may be considered.

Consult before cosmetics

Every permanent makeup service starts with a free consultation.

Come ready

No sunburn or fresh tan, disclose any skin conditions, and arrive sober — the studio may refuse service otherwise.
