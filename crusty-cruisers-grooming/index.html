Meet the groomer

Built for the old souls.

Ivy has been grooming pets for 10 years. Her love for senior pets started at home, with her own dogs Spittles and Murdock, who needed extra care and patience as they got older.

That patience is the whole idea behind Crusty Cruisers: a cozy grooming bus that comes to you, so older and anxious pets skip the car ride and the busy salon. Ivy's fiancé, Hunter, backed the mobile idea from day one.

"We will always prioritize the pet's welfare, safety, and comfort over style preferences."From the Crusty Cruisers policies

Good to know

Before your first groom.

The short version of our policies, so booking is easy.

First-visit deposit

First-time clients pay a $50 deposit to hold their appointment slot. It's credited toward the final groom price.

Vaccinations

Proof of up-to-date vaccinations is needed 48 hours before service, or the appointment will be canceled.

Cancellations

Please give 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations carry a $50 fee.

Be home on arrival

If no one is present when the bus arrives, the appointment is canceled with a fee.

No sedation

Crusty Cruisers never sedates pets, since we aren't licensed veterinarians.

Pregnant & nursing pets

To avoid stress risks, we don't groom pregnant or nursing pets.
