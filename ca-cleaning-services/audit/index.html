C&A Cleaning Services

We read through your Wix site on November 18, 2026, the way someone in Cleveland looking for a cleaner would. Your services are laid out clearly (residential, commercial, move in/out and organizing), and Your Satisfaction Is Our Priority is a motto people remember. Here are a few things that would help the site work as hard as you do.

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1The Book Online page says there's nothing to book

What we saw

Book Online is one of only two items in your menu. Clicking it opens a page that says Nothing to book right now. Check back soon. No services are listed there.

Why it matters

Someone who clicks “Book Online” is ready to hire you. Being told there's nothing to book can make them think you're not taking new clients.

Quick fix

Either add your services in Wix Bookings, or remove the page from the menu and replace it with a “Call or text for a quote” button.

2Two phone numbers and two email addresses

What we saw

The top of every page shows 423-457-0537 and Angfain78@gmail.com. The contact section and footer show (423) 641-6076 and Clevelandhomecleaning@gmail.com.

Why it matters

A customer can't tell which number or inbox is the right one, and if one isn't checked often, a new job could slip through.

Quick fix

Pick the one number and one email you want customers to use and show only those, in the header and the footer.

3The site uses two different business names

What we saw

The header reads ANGELA'S CLEANING SERVICES and the intro says We're Angela's Cleaning Services, while the services, the footer and the browser tab say C&A Cleaning Services LLC.

Why it matters

When people search for you or look up reviews, they need one name to look for. Two names can make it seem like two different companies.

Quick fix

Choose the name you want customers to know you by and use it everywhere. If both matter, a line like “C&A Cleaning Services LLC, also known as Angela's Cleaning Services” keeps it clear.

4The specials don't say when or what they cover

What we saw

The Specials section offers This Month only: $100 for a standard cleaning on a home up to 1500 sq ft without naming the month. Further down, a SALE heading shows $30/Hour with no description of what that rate is for.

Why it matters

Customers may call expecting a price that's no longer offered, or skip calling because they aren't sure what the price includes.

Quick fix

Add an end date to the monthly special (or remove it when it's over), and add one line under “$30/Hour” saying which service it applies to.

5A leftover “Heading 2” placeholder

What we saw

Just below Contact Us and above the GET TO KNOW US section, the page shows the words Heading 2.

Why it matters

It's a small thing, but unedited template text is the kind of detail people notice when they're deciding who to let into their home.

Quick fix

Click that text box in the Wix editor and delete it, or replace it with a real heading.

6A Wix ad banner on top and a 2017 footer

What we saw

The first thing on the page is a Wix banner (Create your website today.Start Now), and the footer reads ©2017 BY C&A CLEANING SERVICES LLC. PROUDLY CREATED WITH WIX.COM. The address is clevelandhomeclean.wixsite.com/cleaningmaideasy.

Why it matters

The banner pushes your name down and invites visitors to click away, and an old year can make people wonder whether you're still taking customers. A long wixsite address is also hard to remember or print on a card.

Quick fix

Update the footer to 2026, and if you can, connect a short domain of your own and remove the free-plan banner.

What's working

  • Your services are easy to follow. Residential, commercial, move in/out cleaning and organizing each get their own short description.
  • You show real prices. $20 an hour for organizing tells people what to expect before they call.
  • You say where you work. Cleveland, TN, USA and the Cleveland and surrounding areas are right on the page, and your Facebook page is linked.
