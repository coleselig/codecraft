from a blog to a bakery

"Baking is a ministry." Heidi Davison, head baker & owner, with her husband Dan

Heidi's Home Cooking started as a food blog that grew to over 1.3 million readers a year. When the custom orders wouldn't stop, Heidi and Dan bought a building on Greenway Drive and spent 16 months turning it into the bakery it is today.

2015

Heidi launches her food blog.

2017

Requests for custom bakes start rolling in.

Dec 2021

The Greenway Drive building is bought, and a 16-month renovation begins.

April 2024

Heidi's Bakery opens its doors in Fountain City.
