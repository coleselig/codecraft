More than a farm

A place to learn the work.

Vuck Farm describes itself as a Farm Entrepreneur Incubator. The goal is hands-on: growing food and herbs the biodynamic way, and teaching the skills behind it.

"We offer the products of these efforts at the Farmers Market and through our CSA."

Biodynamic market gardening

Experiential, on-the-ground education in growing for market the biodynamic way.

Herb production

Fresh and dried herbs, medicinal herbs and salves, plus edible and dried flowers.

Sustainable living skills

The everyday know-how that keeps a small farm, and a household, resilient.

The CSA

Twelve weeks of the garden.

The CSA runs on 12-week commitments, with vegetables from the farm and pickup in Knoxville. ASAP's 2026 Tennessee CSA chart lists Vuck Farm with a fall/winter option too.
