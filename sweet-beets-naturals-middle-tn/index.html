How it's grown and made

Clean food, healthy land, good community

The farm side of Sweet Beets is about clean food, healing the land, and supporting the community.

No-till beds

Regenerative methods: no tilling, crop rotation and intercropping.

Worm & tea composts

Soil fed with compost instead of artificial chemical fertilizers.

Diversity over spray

Diverse garden beds help keep pests down, with no artificial chemical pest control.

Natural materials

Handcrafted pieces made from natural materials, one order at a time.
