Why in-home

Take the overwhelm out of grooming day.

For many dogs, a busy grooming shop is sensory overload. Bringing the groom home removes those triggers.

A busy salon can mean

  • Loud noises and strange smells
  • Harsh fluorescent lighting
  • Unfamiliar animals and people
  • Time waiting in a kennel

A Sirius groom at home

  • Your dog's own space and routine
  • One groomer, one dog, full attention
  • No kennel time, no drop-off or pickup
  • Reiki relaxation to ease anxiety
