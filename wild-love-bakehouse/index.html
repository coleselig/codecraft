The Bake Case

What’s coming out of the oven.

Everything is made in small batches, from scratch, every day. The case changes with the seasons — here’s what you’ll usually find.

The house specialty

Croissants & laminated pastries

Flaky, buttery and baked in small batches each morning — the pastry Wild Love is known for. Seasonal pastries rotate through the case alongside them.

Grab & go

Pies & tarts

Pies and tarts — grab one from the case, or watch for seasonal pre-orders around the holidays.

Celebrate

Cakes

A growing lineup of scratch-made cakes — for birthdays, Sunday dinners and no reason at all.

Sweet tooth

Cookies

From-scratch cookies — the perfect thing to tuck in a bag for later.

At the bar

Coffee, espresso & tea

A rotating single-origin coffee on batch and manual brew, espresso drinks, and loose-leaf tea.

Seasonal

Holiday pre-orders

When the holidays roll around, Wild Love takes pre-orders for pies, tarts and seasonal bakes. Call the shop to ask what’s on the list this year.

The case changes daily and sells out — call ahead at (865) 200-8078 if you have your heart set on something.
