How we grow

Leave the soil alone, and it feeds you back

"We're a no-till market garden rooted in healthy soil." Everything else follows from that.

No-till beds

The soil isn't turned over, so its structure and life stay intact. The goal is nutrient-rich, flavorful vegetables.

Grown for these mountains

Crops chosen to suit the Appalachian climate, from Thai basil and sugar snap peas to pawpaws and figs.

Organic practices

Organic (not certified), low spray and regenerative, as listed with ASAP's Appalachian Grown guide.

Local kitchens trust it

Golden Roots supplies Timber! as well as selling at the Elizabethton Farmers Market.
