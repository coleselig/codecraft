Good to Know

Before You Book

Is the deposit an extra cost?

Not at all! A deposit is required to start work on your tattoo, but it applies to your final tattoo cost.

Can minors get pierced?

Select piercings can be done on minors, but the standard answer is 18.

What ID do I need?

A valid government or state issued driver's license, ID, or passport.

Can you swap my jewelry?

Yes! There's a small fee: $20 for up to 4 pieces.

Do consultations cost anything?

No. Tattoo consultations, piercing checks and anatomy consults are all free.
