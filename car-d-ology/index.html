Intake · Describe the symptom

Something feels off? Start with the symptom.

You don't need to know what's broken. Tell us what the car is doing, and a precision diagnosis comes before any parts get swapped.

SYMPTOM / 01

A warning light came on

Check-engine or dash lights are the car asking for a diagnosis. Call and tell us what you're seeing.

SYMPTOM / 02

A new noise or vibration

Grinding, squealing, clunking or shaking. Describe when it happens and we'll trace it to the source.

SYMPTOM / 03

It's running hot or rough

Overheating, hesitation, hard starts or poor idle. Pull over safely, then give us a call.

SYMPTOM / 04

Brakes or steering feel wrong

Soft pedal, pulling to one side, or a wheel that sounds off. Safety first: call before you drive far.

SYMPTOM / 05

Shifting isn't smooth

Slipping, hard shifts or delayed engagement. Note what you feel and when, and bring it in.

SYMPTOM / 06

Not sure what it is

That's what diagnostics are for. A quick call is the fastest way to find out what your car needs.
