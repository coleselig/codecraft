Organic greens, stone-ground flour & farm yarn from Bradley County.

Red Clay Farm grows certified organic produce, mills its own organic grains into flour, cornmeal and grits, and spins fiber from its alpacas, llamas, Jacob sheep and Angora goats. You'll find the stall at Chattanooga-area farmers markets.
