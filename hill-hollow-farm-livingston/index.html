A whole county's worth of makers.

From Hill Hollow Farm

And from our own fields in Overton County.

Hill Hollow started as a family farm before it was ever a storefront. We grow fresh produce without pesticides or sprays, make our own jams and jellies, and raise all-natural meat and livestock.

Why Hill Hollow

A family farm that became a gathering place.

Matthew and Kelly Hill bought land in Overton County looking for a quieter way of life, and started what is now Hill Hollow Farm. In 2024 they opened the market on East Main so local makers would have a place to sell.

01

Every product is vetted

Kelly checks what goes on the shelves, so customers don't have to read every label closely.

02

Built for cottage makers

The market was made as a home for cottage businesses selling handmade products, and now stocks goods from 76 vendors.

03

Run by family

Matthew and Kelly run the market themselves, with Kelly's mom helping out.
