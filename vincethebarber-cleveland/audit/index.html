Vince the Barber

We went through vincethebarber1790.com and your Square booking page on November 18, 2026, the way a new customer in Cleveland would. What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves today. Most take a few minutes.

1Google and shared links still show your old address

What we saw

Your page says I am now at a new location! and lists 2080 Chambliss Ave NW, Cleveland, TN 37311. But the page title and link-preview title are still Vince The Barber 1790 | Village Center | 201 Keith Street Southwest, Cleveland, TN, USA.Home page: vincethebarber1790.com

Why it matters

The title is what shows on the browser tab, usually as the headline when your site comes up in Google, and when someone texts your link to a friend. People can see Keith Street before they ever open the page.

Easy fix

In Wix, open the page's SEO settings and change the title to something like “Vince the Barber | Copperhead Barber Co, 2080 Chambliss Ave NW, Cleveland TN”. Update the social share title too.

2Saturday hours end before they start

What we saw

Your booking page lists Sat: 8:00 PM - 4:00 PM.Square booking page: square.site/book/HJGG3CGVB4X7E/vincethebarber-cleveland-tn

Why it matters

A customer planning a Saturday cut can't tell whether you open at 8 in the morning or at night.

Easy fix

In Square, go to Settings → Locations → Business hours and change Saturday to 8:00 AM (or whatever is right).

3Prices on the site don't match the booking page

What we saw

Your site lists Haircut $25, Deluxe Beard Service $25 and Beard Trim 16$.Home page: vincethebarber1790.com

Your booking page lists Haircut $26, Deluxe Beard Service $26 and Basic Beard Trim Clippers Only Beard Trim $18.Square booking page: square.site/book/HJGG3CGVB4X7E/vincethebarber-cleveland-tn

Why it matters

It's a small difference, but customers notice when the price at checkout isn't the one they saw first.

Easy fix

Pull services and prices from one place. The simplest option is to let the site show your Square menu, so it stays up to date by itself.

4Your social icons lead to Wix, not to you

What we saw

The Facebook, Twitter and LinkedIn icons link to facebook.com/wix, twitter.com/Wix and linkedin.com/company/wix-com, the website builder's own accounts.Home page: vincethebarber1790.com

Why it matters

Someone who taps them to see your cuts ends up on a software company's page.

Easy fix

Remove those three and keep your Instagram, which already links to your real account.

5No hours or phone number on the site

What we saw

The site is a single page. We didn't find opening hours or a phone number on it, only the button text Book with Vince: Click the link below.Home page: vincethebarber1790.com

Why it matters

Somebody who just wants to know whether you're in today has to leave the site and dig through the booking page.

Easy fix

Put your days and hours (and a phone number or text line, if you take calls) right under the address.

6The footer still says 2023

What we saw

The footer reads ©2023 by Vince The Barber. Proudly created with Wix.com.Home page: vincethebarber1790.com

Why it matters

Together with the old title, an old year makes people wonder whether the rest of the page is current, even though your address is.

Easy fix

Update it to 2026, or have it update automatically each year.

What's working

  • You told people about the move. The page clearly says you're at a new location and gives the full Copperhead Barber Co address on Chambliss Ave.
  • Booking is one tap away. The Book button goes straight to your live Square calendar for the Chambliss Ave location.
  • Your Instagram link works and goes to your real account (@vincethebarber1790), and your service descriptions, like the hot towel, hot lather and straight razor on the Deluxe Beard Service, tell people exactly what they're getting.
