No. 03

A family craft, reimagined

Westside Luxuries comes from Lloyd and Gail Harris, lifelong Cookeville locals and the family behind Harris Monuments. The boutique carries the same craftsmanship and care into the pieces people bring home.

Fine fragrances share the room with hand-poured candles, earth-inspired décor, one-of-a-kind granite and original art by Denice Taylor Rinks. It's everyday luxury, under one roof on West Spring Street.
