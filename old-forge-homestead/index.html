Herdshare: how it works

Own a share of the herd. Get your milk weekly.

Old Forge Homestead offers herdshare memberships for raw goat milk. Here's the whole thing in three steps.

1Join

Pay a one-time seasonal joining fee

That makes you a member of the herdshare for the season.

2Choose

Pick a full or half gallon share

Your weekly fee is based on your share size.

3Collect

Pick up at the farm, or get it delivered to Johnson City

Pickup days are worked out with each member. Johnson City delivery costs a small fee.

Ask about current fees and openings. Just call (423) 518-0155.

The farm

Run by Donica & Caleb.

They met as camp counselors, leading campers on hikes along the Appalachian Trail. Now they raise dairy goats in Carter County.

  • Veteran and woman ownedThey describe the farm as "USMC Disabled Veteran Owned, & Woman Owned."
  • Non-GMO feed, rotational pastureAll the livestock eat non-GMO feed from local mills and graze on rotational pasture.
  • Rooted in the Elizabethton Farmers MarketDonica helped launch the market, and you can find the farm there.
