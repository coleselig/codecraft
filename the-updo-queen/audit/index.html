Free website check-up for The Updo Queen

We looked at the1updoqueen.wixsite.com/the-updo-queen and its Event Prep, About and Contact pages, plus your Square booking page, on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1A Wix ad sits at the top of every page

Saw This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button that links to Wix. A version of it showed on all four pages we checked.

Cost The first button a visitor sees sends them to Wix's sign-up page instead of to your booking link.

Fix Move to a site without the ad banner and put your Book Now button in that spot.

2Your phone number isn't on the home page

Saw The home page has two Book Now buttons but no phone number. (931)261-9467 only appears on the Contact page, and the Event Prep and About pages don't show it either.

Cost Brides and pageant moms often want to ask a quick question first. If they can't see a number, some will move on to the next stylist.

Fix Add a tap-to-call button in the header so your number is one tap away on every page.

3No hours anywhere on the site

Saw We didn't find business hours on the Home, Event Prep, About or Contact pages.

Cost People planning around a prom or wedding date can't tell when you're available without reaching out first.

Fix Add your hours (or "by appointment") next to your address and phone.

4Two different ZIP codes for the same address

Saw The home page says 303 S. Willow Ave. Cookeville TN, 38570, while the Contact page says Cookeville TN, 38501 (your Square page also uses 38501).

Cost Mismatched details can confuse map apps and make Google less sure which listing is correct.

Fix Use one address, word for word, everywhere it appears.

5Photo descriptions are file names

Saw Photos on the Event Prep page are labeled with names like IMG_2549_edited.jpg and pageant blue sitting_edited.jpg.

Cost Google and screen readers use these labels to understand your photos, so they're missing a chance to say "bridal updo in Cookeville".

Fix Give each photo a short, plain description of the style shown.

What's working

Your services are clearly laid out with real descriptions (Event Hairstyling, Airbrush Makeup, The Queen's Prep, Vintage Hairdressing and more), there's a real review from Amanda S. (Amazing experience! So relaxing! 10 out of 10 recommend!), and the Book Now buttons go straight to your Square booking. That's a strong base.
