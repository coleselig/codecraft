Free website check-up for eGreen Lawn Pros

We looked at james10267.wixsite.com/egreen-lawn-pros-1, its Book Online and Contact pages, on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1Two “Book Now” buttons don't lead to a booking

Saw On the home page and the Book Online page, Spring Yard Cleanup and Leaf Removal / Fall Clean-up each show a Book Now button, and both buttons link to #. Your other services have More Info buttons that open their own pages.

Cost Someone who is ready to book a cleanup taps the button and nothing happens, right at the moment they wanted to hire you.

Fix Point both buttons at a real booking page, or change them to a simple “Call or email for a quote” button with your number.

2Your phone number is only on the Contact page

Saw The home page, About Us and Book Online pages list only james@egreenlawnpros.com. The number 4236676189 appears only on the Contact Us page.

Cost Most people looking for lawn care want to call or text. If they don't spot a number on the first page, many will move on to the next company.

Fix Put a tap-to-call number in the header of every page, written in a readable format like (423) 667-6189.

3A small typo on a service name

Saw On the Book Online page, one service is spelled Lawn Fertiliztion.

Cost It's minor, but typos in a service name can make a careful customer wonder about attention to detail.

Fix Correct it to “Lawn Fertilization” (the page address /service-page/lawn-fertiliztion has the same spelling).

4A Wix ad sits at the top of the site

Saw The home, About Us and Contact pages show This site was designed with the .com website builder. Create your website today. with a Start Now button that links to Wix.

Cost The first button a visitor sees sends them to Wix's sign-up page instead of to you.

Fix Move to a site without the ad banner and use that spot for “Call for a free quote”.

5Footer still says ©2023

Saw ©2023 by eGreen Lawn Pros.

Cost An old date can make people wonder whether the business is still taking customers.

Fix Show the current year, along with your phone number and service area.

What's working

Your story is clear and different: Chattanooga's Premier All-Electric, All Solar-Powered lawn care service. You have three real customer testimonials (Allen C, Justin G, Chris H, from Hixson and Chattanooga), a full list of eight services, and your address on the Contact page. That's a strong base to build on.
