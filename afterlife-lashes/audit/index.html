Afterlife Lashes

We read through your website on November 18, 2026, the way a new client in Knoxville looking for lash extensions would. Your reviews are lovely and your credentials are clear. Here are a few small things that would make it easier for that client to book with you.

Checked November 18, 20264 things to tidy upNo cost, no strings

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1The booking buttons point to a page under a different name

What we saw

Both ONLINE BOOKING in the menu and the Online booking button under Book with me link to square.site/book/FRWZHXDSKXG8R/asia-kay-beauty-knoxville-tn. Your current booking page, afterlifelashes.glossgenius.com, isn’t linked anywhere on the Wix site.

Why it matters

We know Asia Kay is where your lash certification comes from, but a first-time client may not. Seeing a different business name when they go to book could make them wonder whether they’re in the right place, right at the moment they’re ready to commit.

Easy fix

Point both booking buttons to your GlossGenius page so clients book straight into the calendar you use today.

2No prices on the website

What we saw

The Wix site shows no services or prices. Your GlossGenius page lists 12 services with prices, for example Classic Full Set $140, Hybrid Full Set $160 and Volume Full Set $200.

Why it matters

Price is one of the first things people check when choosing a lash artist. When it’s missing, many will keep scrolling to someone whose prices they can see.

Easy fix

Add a short services list with your full-set and fill prices (or a “See services & prices” button to GlossGenius).

3Nothing says where you are or how to call

What we saw

The home and About pages show no phone number, address or hours. The page title is Lash Artist | Afterlife Lashes | United States, and the only mention of Knoxville is inside the booking link’s address.

Why it matters

Someone searching for lashes in Knoxville can’t tell from the site that you’re local, and a client who wants to ask a quick question has only email or a form.

Easy fix

Add your studio location, the phone number from your GlossGenius page (865-761-9005) and your hours to the “Contact me” section, and change “United States” in the page title to “Knoxville, TN”.

4A Wix ad is the first thing visitors see

What we saw

The top of both pages shows the banner This site was designed with the .com website builder. Create your website today. with a Start Now link to Wix.

Why it matters

For a beauty business, the look of the site is part of the first impression, and an ad for someone else’s product sits above your name.

Easy fix

A paid Wix plan removes the banner and lets you use your own domain, or move to a site that includes one.

What's working

  • Five real client reviews. Lines like “I got my makeup done by Lexi and she did an AMAZING job. Highly recommend” (Megan P.) are exactly what a nervous first-timer wants to read.
  • Your credentials are front and center. The About page clearly says you’re a licensed esthetician, a Douglas J Aveda graduate and certified in classic and volume.
  • Your own socials and email are linked. The Instagram, Facebook and email links all go to your accounts, and there’s a gallery of your work.
