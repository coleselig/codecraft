Two hundred years on the ridge.

Hudson Farms is a small, family-run farm on Walden's Ridge. The family has farmed this land for over 200 years, and the goal is simple: fresh, healthy produce.

The family farm

Small farm, long roots.

Hudson Farms has been selling at local farmers markets, restaurants and grocery stores since 2008. The farm grows berries, melons and a long list of vegetables, and its practices are listed as GMO-free.
