Cleaning with Integrity

We read through your Wix site on November 18, 2026, the way an office manager looking for a cleaning crew would. What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1The Services page still shows template placeholder text

What we saw

Under OUR SERVICES, the two service headings both read SERVICE NAME, followed by generic lines like We strive to make things hassle-free and convenient, especially when it comes to this service. The page never names an actual service.

Why it matters

Anyone who taps “Services” in your menu expects to see what you offer. A page of placeholders can make it look like the site was never finished.

Quick fix

Replace the two placeholders with your real packages (Welcome Package, Premium Perfection, Show Room Stopper), which you already describe well on your home page.

2Your email address is printed wrong on the Contact page

What we saw

The Contact page shows your email as Cleaningwithintegrityllc@gmail.com@gmail.com, with “@gmail.com” twice. (Tapping it still opens the correct address; the visible text is what's off.)

Why it matters

People who copy or type the address they see will send to an address that doesn't exist, and you'll never know they tried.

Quick fix

Edit that text box and delete the extra “@gmail.com”.

3The reviews section has no reviews in it

What we saw

The home page has a section headed DONT FORGET TO "RATE OUR SERVICES" with How Did We Do? and Testimonials. How was your clean?, but below that there are only photos: no review text and no customer names.

Why it matters

A business choosing a cleaner is handing over the keys to its space, so words from real clients carry a lot of weight. An empty testimonials heading is a missed chance.

Quick fix

Add two or three short quotes from happy clients (with their permission), or a link to where people can leave you a review.

4A Wix ad banner sits at the top of every page

What we saw

The first thing on the home page is This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button, and every footer reads Proudly created with Wix.com. The address is emoulder7609.wixsite.com/cleaningwithintegrit.

Why it matters

The banner pushes your name down and invites visitors to click away. A long wixsite address (with “integrity” cut short) is also hard to say out loud, print on a card or remember.

Quick fix

Connect your own domain (something short like your business name) and remove the free-plan banner.

5The phone number is hard to read and mostly can't be tapped

What we saw

In the header, footer and on the Contact page the number appears as 8652811852, with no spaces or dashes, as plain text. The only tap-to-call spot we found was the Get in Touch button on the home page.

Why it matters

Ten digits run together are easy to misdial, and on a phone people expect to tap a number to call it.

Quick fix

Write it as (865) 281-1852 and link it to tel:+18652811852 everywhere it appears.

6A few typos in the headlines and package lists

What we saw

The tagline under your name on every page reads Knoxvilles most trusted cleaning services. The home page also has Your cleaning specialist’s, Moping in both package lists, 10am-6.pm in your hours, and how service’s can benefit your facility.

Why it matters

For a business whose name is a promise about care and detail, small slips like these are what a picky client notices first.

Quick fix

Change them to “Knoxville's”, “Your cleaning specialists”, “Mopping”, “10am-6pm” and “services”.

7The footer still says 2022

What we saw

Every page we checked ends with ©2022 by Cleaning with Integrity.

Why it matters

An old year can make people wonder whether the business is still active and taking new clients.

Quick fix

Update it to 2026, or set it to update automatically each year.

What's working

  • Your packages are spelled out. The Welcome Package and Premium Perfection list exactly what's included, from high dusting to Stainless steel shine and baseboards.
  • Your hours are clear. Office hours are Monday-Friday 10 to 6, with cleaning available 24/7, right on the home page.
  • It's easy to ask for a quote. The GET A QUOTE form with Fair Prices, Guaranteed is on the home page, and the Contact page gives your Clinton address.
  • Your voice comes through. The welcome message about working side by side with your cleaners and building trust sounds like a real person, not a template.
