Free website check-up

Atkins Body Shop

We read through your website the way someone in Monroe County with a dented fender or a car in the ditch would. Your address, email and history are all there, which is great. Here are a few small things that would make the site look as established as a shop that's been around since 1962.

Checked November 18, 20265 things to tidy upNo cost, no strings

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site on November 18, 2026. Most are quick fixes you can make in the Wix editor.

1The browser tab says “Mysite” instead of your name

What we saw

The page title, shown on the browser tab and used as the headline when the page is shared or bookmarked, is HOME | Mysite. The site name is also set to Mysite, and the web address ends in /mysite.

Why it matters

Anyone who saves or shares your page sees “Mysite” rather than Atkins Body Shop, and the title doesn't mention body work, towing or Madisonville.

Easy fix

In Wix's site and SEO settings, change the name and home page title to something like “Atkins Body Shop | Body Shop & Wrecker Service, Madisonville TN.”

2The footer credits “Jade&Andy”

What we saw

The bottom of the page reads © 2023 by Jade&Andy. Proudly created with Wix.com.

Why it matters

That's the name left over from the Wix template. A family business with six decades of history deserves its own name there, and a stranger may wonder who Jade and Andy are.

Easy fix

Change it to “© 2026 Atkins Body Shop.”

3The Facebook, Pinterest and Instagram icons go to Wix

What we saw

The footer icons link to facebook.com/wix, pinterest.com/wixcom and instagram.com/wix, the website builder's own accounts.

Why it matters

Customers who tap them to see your work end up on a software company's page.

Easy fix

Link the ones you actually use to your own pages and delete the rest.

4Two phone numbers and no hours

What we saw

The header shows 423-442-3860 and the Find Us section shows Tel: 1(423) 261-4156, with nothing saying which to use. We didn't find business hours anywhere on the page.

Why it matters

Someone who needs a tow or wants to drop off a car has to guess which number to call and whether you're open.

Easy fix

Label each number (for example “Shop” and “Wrecker”, if that's how you use them), make both tap-to-call, and add your hours.

5A Wix ad is the first thing visitors see

What we saw

The top of the page shows the banner This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button.

Why it matters

The very first button on your site advertises another company.

Easy fix

A paid Wix plan removes the banner and lets you use your own domain name, like your shop's name dot com.

What's working

  • Your history is front and center. Atkins Body Shop began serving East Tennessee in 1962. and We are a family-owned and operated business are exactly what people want to read before trusting someone with their car.
  • Insurance made easy. We welcome all insurance claims and can handle the paperwork for you in our office answers one of the first questions after an accident.
  • Clear about what you do. Unlocks, tire changes, jump starts, winch-out recovery, towing and body repair are all listed, along with your address at 615 Anderson St, Madisonville.
