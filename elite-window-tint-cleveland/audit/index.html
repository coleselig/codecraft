Free website check-up for Elite Window Tint & Detail

We looked at elitewindowtinting4.wixsite.com/my-site and its Get a Quote page on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1The footer names a different business

Saw © 2035 by Direct Automotives. Powered and secured by Wix on both pages we checked.

Cost A customer who notices another company's name, and a year that hasn't happened yet, may wonder whose site they're on.

Fix Change it to "© 2026 Elite Window Tint & Detail" with your address and phone.

2The Get a Quote page mentions a form that isn't there

Saw The page says FILL OUT THE FORM BELOW AND WE WIL BE IN TOUCH SHORTLY., but there is no form below it. The only way to ask for a quote is Tap the link to get a quote, which opens an outside quote tool.

Cost Someone expecting a form may scroll, find nothing, and leave before they find the link.

Fix Change the heading to match the link ("Tap below to get your quote"), fix "WIL", and make the link a big button.

3The number of years is missing

Saw The home page says With multiple years of experience without saying how many years.

Cost A real number builds trust, and "multiple" reads like a blank that was never filled in.

Fix Put in your actual years in business or number of vehicles tinted.

4A stray "BUY- NEW" heading

Saw On the home page, a heading reads BUY- NEW above the line about checking out your latest tinting and detailing packages.

Cost It's unclear what it means, so it can look like leftover template text.

Fix Replace it with something clear, like "New tint & detail packages".

5A Wix ad sits at the top of the page

Saw This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button that links to Wix.

Cost The first button a visitor sees sends them to Wix's sign-up page instead of to you.

Fix Move to a site without the ad banner and put "Get a Quote" in that spot.
