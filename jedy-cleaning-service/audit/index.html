Free website check-up for JEDY Cleaning Service

We looked at jcsknoxvilletn.wixsite.com/website and its Get a Quote page on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1No list of what you clean

Saw The home page headline is BOOK A TRUSTED MAID INSTANTLY, but neither the home page nor the Get a Quote page lists any services (for example regular, deep or move-out cleaning). The quote form just asks visitors to Please Explain Below.

Cost A visitor can't tell whether you do the kind of cleaning they need, so they have to call or guess before asking for a quote.

Fix Add a short services list with one line on each, and turn it into a dropdown on the quote form.

2No service area named

Saw Neither page names a city or county you serve. The booking form has a CITY field, but the site never says where you work.

Cost Someone outside your area may fill out the form for nothing, and someone inside it can't be sure you'll come to them. Google also has less to go on when people search for cleaners near them.

Fix Add a line like “Serving Knoxville and nearby areas” near the top of the home page and in the footer.

3“Get In Touch” sends people to Facebook

Saw The Get In Touch button links to facebook.com/JEDY-Cleaning-Service-106437578586613 instead of to a form or your phone number.

Cost Visitors who were ready to contact you leave your site, and some won't find their way back.

Fix Point the button to your quote form or make it a tap-to-call button for 8659736692.

4A Wix ad sits at the top of the site

Saw The home page shows This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button that links to Wix.

Cost The first button a visitor sees sends them to Wix's sign-up page instead of to you.

Fix Move to a site without the ad banner and use that spot for “Get a free quote”.

5Footer still says ©2021

Saw ©2021 by JEDY Cleaning Service.

Cost A five-year-old date can make people wonder whether you're still in business.

Fix Show the current year, along with your phone number and service area.

What's working

Your phone number is a tap-to-call link right in the menu (CALL 8659736692), you have a quote form that confirms We'll send you a price quote soon, and the trust section speaks to a real worry: We know inviting someone into your home is a big deal. You also have a gallery of your cleaning work and a Rate Our Service form. That's a solid base to build on.
