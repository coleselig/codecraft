Free website check-up

The Laptop Dudes

We read through thelaptopdudes.com on November 18, 2026, page by page, the way someone in Cookeville with a broken laptop would. Your free estimates and starting prices are clear and easy to find, which is great. Here are a few small things that would help people get in touch with you.

Checked November 18, 20265 things to tidy upNo cost, no strings

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1The contact form doesn’t seem to have any boxes to fill in

What we saw

On the Contact page we found a Send button and the message Your details were sent successfully!, but no fields for a name, email or question.

Why it matters

Someone who prefers to write rather than call has nowhere to type, so that message is lost before it starts.

Easy fix

Rebuild the form with Name, Phone or Email, and “What’s wrong with your device?”, or remove it and keep the call/text/email options that already work.

2The LOCATION section is empty

What we saw

Below the Send button, the Contact page has a LOCATION heading with nothing under it when we checked: no address and no map. (Your address does appear higher up: 39 West Broad St., Suite 1A, Cookeville.)

Why it matters

An empty section looks like something broke, and a map is the easiest way for someone new to find a suite on West Broad St.

Easy fix

Add a Google Map of 39 West Broad St. under that heading, or remove the heading.

3Your hours and address aren’t on the home page

What we saw

The home page shows CALL US 931-229-0853 and ESTIMATES ARE ALWAYS FREE!, but not your hours or address. The hours appear only on the Contact page: Open 9-2 M-F / Please call for apointment (with “appointment” misspelled).

Why it matters

With a 9–2 weekday schedule, people really need to see the hours before driving over. Most visitors land on the home page and may never open Contact.

Easy fix

Put “Mon–Fri 9am–2pm, 39 West Broad St., Cookeville” on the home page and in the footer, and fix the spelling of “appointment”.

4Your domain sends visitors to a Wix address with an ad

What we saw

Typing thelaptopdudes.com redirects to laptopdudes.wixsite.com/laptopdudes, and the top of the pages shows a Wix ad banner (on the home page: This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button).

Why it matters

You already own a good domain, but visitors see it swap for a longer address and an ad for someone else’s product, which can make a careful customer hesitate.

Easy fix

Connect thelaptopdudes.com directly to the site (a paid Wix plan does this and removes the banner), or move to a site that uses your domain.

5The footer still says 2019

What we saw

Every page we checked ends with © 2019 The Laptop Dudes All RIGHTS RESERVED. The inner page titles also read like CONTACT | laptopdudes and REPAIRS & UPGRADES | laptopdudes.

Why it matters

An old year can make people wonder whether the prices and hours are still current.

Easy fix

Update the footer to 2026 and change the site name in Wix to “The Laptop Dudes, Cookeville” so every tab and search result shows it.

What's working

  • Free estimates, front and center. ESTIMATES ARE ALWAYS FREE! on the home page and we will never sell you something you don’t need on the Repairs page are exactly what a worried customer wants to hear.
  • Real starting prices. Laptops starting at just $149 and virus removal starting at $39 let people know what to expect before they call.
  • Easy to call. CALL US 931-229-0853 sits at the top of every page, and the Contact page invites people to call, text, or send us an e-mail.
