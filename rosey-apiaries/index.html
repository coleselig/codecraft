Our story

It started with a couple of swarms.

Tim was given a couple of bee swarms and caught a couple more. That handful of colonies grew into a family bee farm that now keeps hives in three counties.

11

apiaries across Greene, Sullivan and Washington Counties

80–100

hives in all, roughly, tended by the family

2

generations at work: Tim and his daughter Kayla

Tim, the beekeeper

Founded the farm. His favorite part is simply watching the bees work.

Kayla, the candle maker

Turns the wax from the family’s hives into hand-poured beeswax candles.
