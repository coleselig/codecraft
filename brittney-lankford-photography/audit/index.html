What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1Most page titles end in “My Site”

What we saw

Your home page title is great (Brittney Lankford Photography and Video | Wedding Photo and Video Team), but the inner pages read Weddings | Elopements | My Site, Engagement | Couples | My Site, Maternity | My Site, PACKAGES | My Site, VIDEO | My Site and WEDDING GUIDE | My Site.

Why it matters

The page title is what shows on the browser tab and as the headline in search results. “My Site” is the Wix default name, so your business name is missing exactly where a couple would see it.

Easy fix

In Wix, change the site name from “My Site” to “Brittney Lankford Photography” (Settings → Website settings). Every page title updates at once.

2The engagement page address is misspelled

What we saw

The Engagement | Couples menu link opens brittneylankford.wixsite.com/my-site/enagement (missing the first “g”). Packages lives at /services-2.

Why it matters

Couples do share links to your portfolio with each other and their families, and a misspelled address looks like a slip on a business that is all about detail.

Easy fix

Rename the page URL to /engagement and Packages to /packages in each page’s SEO settings. Wix can redirect the old addresses so nothing breaks.

3No link to your Instagram or Facebook anywhere

What we saw

We checked the Home, Weddings | Elopements, Engagement | Couples, Maternity, Packages, Video and Wedding Guide pages and found no social media links on any of them. Your reviews on the home page are Instagram Story graphics (file names like Green Elegant Clients Review Instagram Story), so you clearly use it.

Why it matters

Couples usually want to see recent work and real weddings before they reach out, and social media is where most photographers post it. Right now there is no path from your site to that.

Easy fix

Add your Instagram (and Facebook, if you use it) to the header or footer so it shows on every page.

4Your reviews are images, so their words can’t be read

What we saw

The six review graphics on the home page contain the reviews only as pictures. All six share the same description, Traveling Wedding Photography and Videography Team, so the actual words of each review aren’t on the page as text.

Why it matters

Screen readers can’t read the reviews to visitors who rely on them, search engines can’t see them, and the text can be small to read on a phone.

Easy fix

Keep the graphics if you love them, and add each review as a line of text under it (or as its image description), with the couple’s first names.

5The home page doesn’t say where you’re based

What we saw

The home page says you offer wedding services anywhere in the country and have traveled to 29 states, but doesn’t name a home city. Chattanooga only appears in the hidden search descriptions of inner pages (for example Chattanooga maternity photographer…), not in text a visitor sees.

Why it matters

Maternity, family and senior sessions are usually booked close to home, so local families want to know you’re nearby. Search engines also lean on visible text to understand where a business is.

Easy fix

Add one line near the top, such as “Based in Chattanooga, TN — weddings and elopements anywhere.”
