“Not just a video... it's your love story.”

From their wedding guide

They plan around the light.

The team's own guide is full of advice you only get from people who've photographed a lot of weddings. A few favorites:

  1. Get ready by a window

    A bright, open room with natural light makes details look better and skin look radiant, not yellow.

  2. Mind the sunset

    Start the ceremony about two hours before sunset so there's time for formals before the light fades.

  3. Send the family list early

    About two months out, with people's names instead of “Aunt” or “Cousin”.

  4. Kill the strobe lights

    Ambient light only for the key moments. Blue uplighting can turn a white dress blue.
