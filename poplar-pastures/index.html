How the flock is raised

Moved to fresh grass, so the land can rest.

“We rotationally graze our flock of Katahdin hair sheep on grass.” Katahdins are hair sheep: they shed their coat instead of growing wool.

  • 01
    GrazeThe flock works one paddock of fresh grass.
  • 02
    MoveThey rotate on to the next paddock.

The farm year

Bred in fall, born in spring.

The flock’s calendar, as the farm describes it.

  • FallEwes are bred for spring lambs.
  • WinterTop-quality hay while the grass rests.
  • Pre-lambingGrain for the 4 weeks before lambing, for good nutrition late in pregnancy.
  • Apr – MaySpring lambing.
  • May – AugFacility tours, Monday to Friday.
