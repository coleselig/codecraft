Free website check-up

Divas Lounge

On November 18, 2026 we looked up Divas Lounge the way a new client would: searching your name, tapping your website link, and reading your Square booking page. Your booking setup is solid. Here are a few things that would make it easier for new clients to find you and say yes.

Checked November 18, 20265 things to tidy upNo cost, no strings

What we found

Each item below is something we checked ourselves. Most are quick fixes.

1Your website link leads to a site that no longer exists

What we saw

Your Square booking page lists your website as divas-lounge-brows-lashes.business.site. When we opened that address on November 18, 2026, it returned an error and no website loaded.

Why it matters

Google retired its free business.site websites in 2024, so every one of those addresses stopped working. Anyone who taps “Website” to learn more about you hits a dead end right before booking.

Quick fix

Replace the link with a working site. Until then, point the Website field (on Square and on your Google Business Profile, if it uses the same link) to your Instagram, instagram.com/divas.lounge_, so the tap goes somewhere real.

2People searching for you don't find a Divas Lounge site

What we saw

We searched Divas Lounge Knoxville and Divas Lounge Olde Pioneer Knoxville lashes. The results were other salons and lash studios; none of them was a page of yours.

Why it matters

When someone hears your name from a friend and searches for it, the first thing they find is where they book. Right now that is someone else.

Quick fix

A simple site with your name, “lashes & brows in Knoxville”, your address and a Book button gives search engines something to show for your name.

3The booking page has no photos of your work

What we saw

Your Square booking page lists 24 services and 3 add-ons, but the only images on it are two map pictures. There are no photos of lash sets or brows, and no short “about” text, just the policy notice.

Why it matters

Lash clients choose a stylist by looking at her sets. Without photos, a new customer has to leave the page (and maybe not come back) to see your work.

Quick fix

Add a few of your best Classic, Hybrid and Volume sets to the Square page, plus one or two sentences about you and the studio.

4One service has no price

What we saw

On the booking page, Mini fill in 40 minutes shows a time but no price. Every other fill in lists one.

Why it matters

Clients like to know the cost before they book. A missing price leads to questions or second-guessing.

Quick fix

Add the price to the Mini fill in service in Square.

5Two small spelling mistakes on the menu

What we saw

We noticed Lash removel $30 and Fuller hyrbid fill in on the booking page.

Why it matters

The menu is the part customers read most carefully, so small typos stand out there.

Quick fix

Change removel → removal and hyrbid → hybrid.

What's working

  • Clients can book online. Your Square page lets people pick a service and a time without calling, which many small studios don't offer.
  • Prices and times are posted. Nearly every service shows both, for example lash removal at $30 for 30 minutes.
  • Hours and contact are clear. Mon–Fri 9–3 and 4–6, plus your address, phone (865) 361-3548 and email are all on the page.
  • Your policies are up front. The “please read before booking” notice sets expectations before anyone books.
