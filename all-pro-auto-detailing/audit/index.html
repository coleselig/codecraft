All Pro Auto Detailing

We read through allproautodetailingknoxville.com on November 17, 2026, page by page, with a customer looking for a detail in mind. You clearly do serious work, and the site already has a lot going for it. Here are a few small things that would make it work even harder for you.

Checked November 17, 20265 things to tidy upNo cost, no strings

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site. Most are quick fixes.

1The “Our Reviews” section on the home page is empty

What we saw

On the home page there is a heading Our Reviews, and in the page content we loaded nothing sits under it: no review text and no names. The next thing is the We Also Offer section. (If reviews are supposed to load there through a widget, it was not showing for us.)

Why it matters

An empty reviews heading reads like there are no reviews yet, which undersells a business that mentions a five-star Google rating elsewhere on the same page.

Quick fix

Paste in three or four real Google reviews (with the customer's first name), or reconnect the reviews widget. If neither is handy, remove the heading until it is.

2The contact form only asks for a name and an email

What we saw

On the Contact Us page, the form has two fields, Name and Email*, and a Send button. We did not see a box for a message, the vehicle, or the service needed. The Get A Quote form on the home page also has no message box.

Why it matters

Someone asking about ceramic coating or mold remediation can't tell you what they need, so you have to email back just to find out. Some people give up instead of waiting.

Quick fix

In the form settings, add a “Message” field (and optionally “Vehicle year/make/model” and a service dropdown).

3The Contact page is missing your address and has an empty Photo Gallery

What we saw

The Contact Us page shows your phone number (865-360-2873), but no street address. A Photo Gallery heading near the bottom is followed directly by the copyright line, with no photos under it.

Why it matters

The Contact page is where people go to find where you are. Your address (371 Trane Drive) is already in the home page footer, so it's just not reaching this page. An empty gallery heading looks unfinished.

Quick fix

Add the address and “By appointment only” note to the Contact page, and either upload six to eight before/after photos to the gallery or delete that section.

4Two different numbers for years of experience

What we saw

The Contact Us page says over 10 years of industry experience, while the About Us page says over 13 years of experience.

Why it matters

It's a small thing, but careful customers notice mismatches, and consistency backs up the “professional” message the site works hard to send.

Quick fix

Pick the correct number and use it on both pages.

5A few small typos in headings

What we saw

On the home page: HITEK Premium Window Flim (Film) and ceramic coatings,vehicle restoration (missing space). On the Contact Us page the heading reads Knoxvilles #1 Choice (missing apostrophe; the home page spells it correctly).

Why it matters

Headings are what people skim on a phone, so typos there stand out more than anywhere else, especially on a site selling attention to detail.

Quick fix

Five-minute edit in the GoDaddy editor: fix the three spots above.

What's working

  • Online booking is right at the top. The ONLINE BOOKING CLICK HERE button on the home page sends people straight to your booking page, which is exactly what you want.
  • Service pages are detailed. The HITEK Window Film page, for example, walks through four film options and explains terms like IR, UV and VLT in plain language.
  • Useful extras are easy to find: pick-up and delivery, gift certificates, and the Snap Finance option are all on the home page.
