Our story

Fifty years of farming. A few years of lavender.

Patti and her husband have been farming for over 50 years on two farms in Roane County. They raised show livestock and kept vegetable gardens. Once the kids were grown and moved away, Patti wanted something she could enjoy, and she started with lavender.

  1. 2023

    Started with just 4 lavender plants.

  2. 2024

    Added 50 more plants. Held the first Fall Festival, with more than 100 people there: a chili cook-off, pollinator stations for the kids and a working display hive.

  3. 2025

    Reached around 100 plants and opened the U-pick lavender field in June/July.

  4. 2026 Planned

    A polycarbonate greenhouse that doubles as a retail shop, a lavender processing shop in the barn's tack room, a distilling station for essential oil, and a small lavender festival with classes.
