The Ranch

From pasture & pine thicket to a peaceful country retreat.

Since a dream in 1990

Buck Creek Ranch sits on the outskirts of Cumberland County, in the Homestead community just outside Crossville.

What started as a dream on open pastures and pine thickets is now a quiet country retreat where couples choose their ceremony spot from a variety of options across the grounds, then gather everyone indoors in the Cherokee Room.

And if Tennessee weather has other plans, the Cherokee Room is your indoor option, so the day goes on either way.

Every part of the day, on one property.

Ceremony, getting ready, reception and a place to stay, all at 696 Buck Creek Road.

Ceremony grounds

Pick your spot from a variety of options across the ranch: open backyard lawn, barn, estate grounds or under the trees.

The Cherokee Room

The ranch’s reception hall for receptions, rehearsal dinners, bridal showers and other events, and your rain plan.

Stay & get ready

On-site accommodations and a dressing room, so the wedding party can get ready and stay right where it all happens.
