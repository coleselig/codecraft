How it started

It began with a bus.

Yellowbird Hill started with a converted school bus and a dream of growing fields of flowers. — YELLOWBIRD HILL

Out on the Strawberry Plains property, where the sun sets over House Mountain, the goal is simple and stubborn: use regenerative farming to bring old pasture land back to life, only using what nature provides.

A little love and a lot of work.

What we grow

Flowers first. Good food, too.

Cut and dried flowers, handmade wreaths, farm eggs and a field's worth of vegetables and herbs. What's on the table changes with the season.
