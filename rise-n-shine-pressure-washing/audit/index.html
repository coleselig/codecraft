Rise N Shine Pressure Washing

We read through your Wix site on November 18, 2026, the way a homeowner looking for a pressure washing crew would. Your home page gets straight to the point: it says you've served the Knoxville area since 2022 and sorts your work into three clear groups, homes and patios, vehicles and commercial. Here are a few things that would help the site work as hard as you do.

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1The About page lists a San Francisco address and a fake phone number

What we saw

Under CONTACT US the About page shows Tel. 123-456-7890, Fax. 123-456-7890 and 500 Terry Francois Street, San Francisco, CA 94158. Under VISIT US it lists hours like Monday - Friday 11:00 - 18:30. These are the sample details the Wix template comes with.

Why it matters

A customer who tries that number won't reach you, and a California address makes people wonder if you're really local.

Quick fix

Replace that block with (865) 900-5939, “Serving the Knoxville area” and your real hours (or “By appointment”), or delete it.

2The Services page lists services you don't offer

What we saw

The Our Services page shows six headings: Customer Service Call, Satisfaction Guaranteed, Scheduled Delivery, Quality Assured, Shopping Consultation and Exceptional Service. Pressure washing isn't mentioned on the page.

Why it matters

This is the page people open to see if you can wash their house, driveway or truck. Template headings about delivery and shopping can send them to the next company.

Quick fix

Swap in your three real service lines (Home and Patio, Automotive, Commercial Services) with a sentence or two about what each includes.

3The “Our Story” section is template wording

What we saw

The About page says It all began with a group of people who dreamt of starting something completely new. and We had a great vision, a passion for change and all the right skills. This is the generic text the Wix template comes with.

Why it matters

People hiring someone to come to their home like to know who's showing up. A generic story is easy to skip; a real one builds trust.

Quick fix

Write two or three sentences in your own words: who you are, why you started in 2022 and what kinds of jobs you love doing.

4The social media icons go to Wix, not to you

What we saw

The Facebook, Twitter and LinkedIn icons on every page link to Wix's own accounts (facebook.com/wix, twitter.com/Wix, linkedin.com/company/wix-com).

Why it matters

Someone who taps them hoping to see photos of your work lands on a website builder's page instead.

Quick fix

Point each icon to your own page, or remove the ones you don't use.

5The phone number can't be tapped and is hard to read

What we saw

Your number appears as +18659005939 in plain text (twice on the Contact page, and at the bottom of the About and Services pages). It isn't a tap-to-call link, and there's no contact form on the Contact page.

Why it matters

Most people looking for a pressure washer are on their phone. If they can't tap to call, some won't bother copying eleven digits.

Quick fix

Show it once as (865) 900-5939 and link it to tel:+18659005939 so it calls in one tap.

6A Wix ad banner sits at the top of every page

What we saw

The first thing on the home page is This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button, and the footer reads Proudly created with Wix.com. The address is otkbryan.wixsite.com/website-1.

Why it matters

The banner pushes your name down and invites visitors to click away. An address ending in “website-1” is hard to remember or put on a truck or yard sign.

Quick fix

Connect your own domain (something short like your business name) and remove the free-plan banner.

7The footer still says 2022

What we saw

Every page we checked ends with ©2022 by Rise N Shine Pressure Washing.

Why it matters

An old year can make people wonder whether the business is still active and taking new jobs.

Quick fix

Update it to 2026, or set it to update automatically each year.

What's working

  • You say where and since when. Rise N Shine Pressure Washing opened its doors in 2022 and has served the Knoxville area since, right on the home page.
  • Your services are grouped simply. Home and Patio, Automotive and Commercial Services tell visitors at a glance what you do.
  • Your email is one tap away. PWRiseNShine@gmail.com opens a new email when tapped.
