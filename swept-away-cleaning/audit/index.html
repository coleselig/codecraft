What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site. Most are quick fixes.

1Your phone number is hard to read at a glance

What we saw

In the Contact section, your number is written as 8656790719, ten digits run together with no spaces or dashes.

Why it matters

Your site asks visitors to call us today to book an introductory meeting. A number that's easy to read, with a big tap-to-call button, makes that one step easier.

Quick fix

Write it as (865) 679-0719 and add a “Call now” button linked to tel:+18656790719.

2A Wix ad shows on your site, and the address is long

What we saw

A banner reads This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button that sends visitors to wix.com. The site lives at sweptawaycs1.wixsite.com/sweptaway.

Why it matters

The ad points visitors away from you, and a long address is hard to say out loud, print on a card or remember when a happy customer recommends you.

Quick fix

Connect your own short domain (something like sweptawayknox.com), which also removes the Wix banner on their paid plans.

3No reviews or service area on the page

What we saw

We didn't find any customer reviews or testimonials, and the only location listed is Knoxville, TN, USA, with no list of the neighborhoods or towns you cover.

Why it matters

Inviting a cleaner into your home takes trust, so people look for what other customers say. A service area also tells someone in Farragut or Powell right away whether you come to them.

Quick fix

Add three or four short quotes from happy clients (with their OK) and a line like “Serving Knoxville, Farragut, Powell…” with the areas you actually cover.

4The footer still says 2019

What we saw

The bottom of the page reads ©2019 by Swept Away. Proudly created with Wix.com.

Why it matters

An old year can make visitors wonder whether the business is still taking new clients.

Quick fix

Update it to 2026 and drop the “Proudly created with Wix.com” line.

What's working

  • Your story is personal. The owner's note, signed Londa Jones, explains why you started Swept Away. People hiring a cleaner want to know who they're letting in.
  • Your cleans are spelled out. Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly, Move-out, Move-in and Deep Cleans are all listed clearly.
  • Your team has names. The Our Team section introduces Bethany, Kaitlyn, Gabby and the rest of the crew, and the pet section tells animal lovers they're in good hands.
