From the crew

Mow like a pro between visits.

Improper mowing is one of the most common causes of lawn problems, and one of the easiest to fix. A few rules we live by:

⅓

The one-third rule

Never remove more than a third of the blade at once. Overgrown? Cut a third, rest 3–4 days, repeat.

01

Height, not calendar

Mow when grass is about a third above its ideal height, not on a fixed day.

02

Don’t scalp it

Cutting too low leaves grass thin and open to heat, drought, pests and disease.

03

Change direction

Vary your pattern so grass stands upright and the soil doesn’t rut.

04

Skip wet grass

Wet lawns cut unevenly and clog the mower. Wait for it to dry.

05

Sharp blades

Dull blades rip grass instead of cutting it. Overlap passes by 3 inches.

Ideal cutting heights

2–3″Fescue, bluegrass, rye
¾–1″Bermuda
