Knoxville Mowing Service

We read through your Wix site on November 18, 2026, the way a homeowner looking for a mowing crew would. Your phone number, email and “Free Estimates” offer are right at the top, which is exactly where they should be. Here are a few things that would help the site work as hard as you do.

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1The “Free Estimates” button leads to a site that no longer exists

What we saw

In the Experience That Shows section, the Free Estimates link points to your old Vistaprint site (knoxvillemowers.vpweb.com/Contact-Us.html). That address now forwards to a Vistaprint page that says Webs is retired.

Why it matters

This is the button a ready-to-hire customer taps. Instead of reaching you, they land on someone else's announcement page.

Quick fix

Point the button to your phone (tel: link) or to the contact section on your home page.

2The Mowing Tips page shows a line of code instead of tips

What we saw

The Mowing Tips page displays <meta name="google-site-verification" content="…" /> as visible text. Apart from the menu and footer, that was the only content we found on the page.

Why it matters

Someone clicking “Mowing Tips” from your menu sees what looks like a broken page, which can make the whole business seem less careful than it is.

Quick fix

Delete that text box (the Google verification belongs in Wix's SEO settings, not on the page) and add a few real tips, or hide the page until it's ready.

3Some wording looks like leftover template text

What we saw

The only review on the site is from Quinn Davis: I found out about Knoxville Mowing with a quick Google search…. It and lines like We work closely with our clients to customize the services we provide… read like the generic sample text Wix templates come with.

Why it matters

Reviews are a big part of how people choose a lawn service, and a review that sounds generic is easy to skip past. Your real customers' words carry much more weight.

Quick fix

Swap in two or three real reviews (with permission, from Google, Yelp or Facebook, where you already have pages) and rewrite the intro in your own voice: veteran-owned, mowing Knoxville since 2011.

4A Wix ad banner sits at the top of every page

What we saw

The first thing on the page is This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. Get Started, and the footer reads Proudly created with Wix.com. The address is knoxvillemowing.wixsite.com/knoxvillemowing.

Why it matters

The banner pushes your business name down and invites visitors to click away. A long wixsite address is also harder to say out loud, print on a truck or remember.

Quick fix

Connect your own domain (a short name like your business name) and remove the free-plan banner.

5The footer still says 2018

What we saw

Both pages we checked end with ©2018 by Knoxville Mowing.

Why it matters

An old year can make people wonder whether the business is still active and taking new customers.

Quick fix

Update it to 2026, or set it to update automatically each year.

6A typo and a stray file name on the home page

What we saw

The About section says No job is to big or to small. Near the top, the text google3351418e960a369e.html appears in the page content.

Why it matters

Small slips like these are what people notice when they're deciding between a few lawn services.

Quick fix

Change it to “No job is too big or too small,” and delete the stray file name.

What's working

  • You're easy to reach. Your phone, (865) 566-4316, and email are at the top of the page, and the phone number taps to call on a phone.
  • Your strengths are up front. In business since 2011, U.S. Veteran Owned and Free Estimates are exactly what homeowners want to see first.
  • You're listed elsewhere too. The site links to your Google Maps, Yelp and Facebook pages, so reviews are already within reach.
