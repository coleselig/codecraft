02The method

Soft where it counts. Power where it's tough.

Blasting everything with high pressure is how siding gets etched and shingles get damaged. Godbey Clean carries both soft wash and power wash equipment and matches the method to the surface.

Soft wash

Gentle on the house. Rough on the algae.

Low pressure plus Godbey Clean's own proprietary soft wash solution, which they describe as gentle and very effective. It deep-cleans siding, roofs and play equipment without forcing water where it doesn't belong.

  • Siding
  • Roofs
  • Playgrounds
  • Building facades
Power wash

Full force for flatwork.

Driveways, walkways and patios can take it, and they need it. Power washing cuts through ground-in dirt and staining on concrete and other hard surfaces.

  • Driveways
  • Walkways
  • Patios
  • Hard surfaces
