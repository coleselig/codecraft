Meet Traci

A salon owner's eye, one chair at a time.

Traci started her career in Southern California and spent most of it in Houston, where she opened SalonTrose in an older house in Montrose. After selling it and living abroad with her family, she landed in the Nashville area and opened her suite in Hendersonville.
