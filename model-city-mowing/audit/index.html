Free website check-up for Model City Mowing

We looked at modelcitymowing.wixsite.com/website and its Get a Quote, Services, Testimonials, About, Recent Work and Meet the Team pages on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1The Testimonials page has no reviews on it

Saw The Testimonials page shows the headings Feedback & Reviews and Straight From The Source, but no reviews, star ratings or customer quotes appear under them. No page we checked mentions Thumbtack.

Cost Someone who taps Testimonials to check you out finds an empty page, which can leave them less sure than before they clicked.

Fix Your Thumbtack profile shows 4.7 from 80 reviews. Put a handful of those reviews on this page and on the home page, with a link to the full profile.

2The home page doesn't say what you do

Saw Besides the menu, the home page text is Model City Mowing - Kingsport, TN, Model Work, Agreeable Prices. and the Contact block. Your services, your 12 years of service in the industry and your photos are all on other pages.

Cost Most visitors only see the first page. If it doesn't say "mowing, landscaping, snow and salt in Kingsport", some will leave before they find out.

Fix Add a short summary of your three services, your years of experience, a few Recent Work photos and a "Get a free estimate" button to the home page.

3A Wix ad sits at the top of every page

Saw This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button on the home page, and This site was designed with the .com website builder. Create your website today. with Start Now on the Services and Testimonials pages.

Cost The first button a visitor sees sends them to Wix's sign-up page instead of to your quote form.

Fix Move to a site without the ad banner and put "Get a Free Estimate" or your phone number in that spot.

4The phone number isn't tap-to-call

Saw On the home page, (828) 702-3072 appears as plain text, while modelcitymowing@gmail.com right below it is a clickable link.

Cost On a phone, a customer has to copy the number by hand to call you, and some won't bother.

Fix Make the number a tap-to-call link, and add a "Call" button that stays on screen on phones.

5The footer still says ©2022

Saw ©2022 by Model City Mowing. Proudly created with Wix.com on every page we checked.

Cost An old date can make people wonder whether the site, and the business, is still active.

Fix Show the current year and replace the Wix line with your service area.

What's working

The Meet the Team page tells a real story (My name is Garrett Davis; I own and operate Model City Mowing.), the Services page covers landscaping, mowing and winter Salt Application and Snow Shoveling, Recent Work has a gallery of 25 photos, and the Get a Quote page has a free-estimate form. Your phone, address and email are on every page. That's a strong base to build on.
