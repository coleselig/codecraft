Free website check-up for Wild Honey Hair Parlor

We looked at stylist325.wixsite.com/wild-honey-hair and its About and Contact pages on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1A Wix ad sits at the top of every page

Saw This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. on the home page, and This site was designed with the .com website builder. Create your website today. with a Start Now button on About and Contact.

Cost The first button a visitor sees sends them to Wix's sign-up page instead of to you.

Fix Move to a site without the ad banner and put "Text to book" in that spot.

2Your address is only on the Contact page

Saw The home page only says A Faith-based salon, in Johnson City, TN. 2203 McKinley Road suite 250 appears only on the Contact page.

Cost A new client checking where you are, or how far the drive is, has to go hunting for it.

Fix Show your address with a map link on the home page.

3Two different ways to book

Saw The home page says To book your appointment or a consult, DM the FB or IG page, or text only. The About page says To make an appointment or a consultation, send a direct email to wildhoneyhairparlor@gmail.com

Cost New clients may not know which way you'll actually see, and a missed message is a missed appointment.

Fix Pick one main way to book and say it the same way on every page.

4The About page email button opens the wrong address

Saw Under Contact on the About page, the text says wildhoneyhairparlor@gmail.com but the link behind it is mailto:info@mysite.com, a Wix placeholder.

Cost Anyone who taps it to email you would be writing to an address that isn't yours.

Fix Point that link to wildhoneyhairparlor@gmail.com.

5Wix's social icons are on your About page

Saw Next to your own Facebook and Instagram, the About page has icons linking to facebook.com/wix, twitter.com/wix, linkedin.com/company/wix-com and instagram.com/wix.

Cost Since you book through DMs, someone tapping the wrong icon ends up on Wix's page and can't message you.

Fix Remove the Wix icons and keep only your own Facebook and Instagram.

6Phone and hours are hard to find

Saw Your number 423-963-5530 is on the home page only, not on the Contact page, and we didn't see hours on any page we checked.

Cost People who go straight to Contact won't find a way to text you.

Fix Put your number (tap to text) and your hours on the Contact page and in the footer.

What's working

Your full service menu is right on the home page, and the About page is warm and personal, with Paige's 16 years in the industry, her training and certifications (Amika Products, Pravana, & Keratin Complex). That's exactly what new clients want to read.
