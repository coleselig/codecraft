Meet the owner

Hi, I'm Paige.

Born and raised in Johnson City, Paige Clark named the parlor after her two daughters, her "Wild Honey." Opening a private suite was her dream, and it's now real: the most personal, safe salon experience she can offer.

She specializes in color, blonding, haircuts and styles for all, and also teaches as an educator at Crown Cutz Academy of Barber & Style, where her suite is located.

Late cancellations

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or change. Changes made with less notice carry an administration fee, and a new slot can't be guaranteed.

Late arrivals

Please arrive on time. If you're more than 15 minutes late, your appointment may need to be rescheduled, so let the parlor know if you're running behind.
