Why Phaewild

Grown for flavor and learning, not mass production

  • Spore to supper

    A mushroom farm and foraging operation in Knoxville that runs the whole process itself.

  • Biodiversity first

    Independent and focused on many varieties, with more in development.

  • Grown with care

    Organic (not certified), GMO-free, IPM, permaculture and regenerative practices.

  • Everyone's welcome

    Takes credit/debit, EBT/SNAP, WIC FMNP, Senior FMNP and SNAP Incentives.

  • Chef-trusted

    Supplies Bistro By the Tracks and Jesse's Butcher Shop.

  • Follow the experiments

    The farm documents its growing experiments on YouTube at Phaewild.
