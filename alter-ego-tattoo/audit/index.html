Alter Ego Tattoo & Piercing

We read through alteregoknox.org on November 18, 2026, page by page, the way someone thinking about their first tattoo or piercing would. You already have your own domain, an honest FAQ and Instagram links for each artist, which a lot of shops don’t. Here are a few small updates that would help the site keep up with the shop.

Checked November 18, 20265 things to tidy upNo cost, no strings

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1The Piercings page is one sentence

What we saw

The Piercings page (alteregoknox.org/blank-4) says Exceptional Service. Every Time. and We pierce everything above the belt., with one photo. There is no information on jewelry, prices, age or ID requirements, or aftercare.

Why it matters

People researching a piercing usually want to know what jewelry you use, roughly what it costs and how to look after it, before they call. Your Tattoos page already covers aftercare, so piercing customers get less to go on.

Easy fix

Add a short list of common piercings with a starting price or “call for pricing”, the jewelry you use, your age/ID policy and a few aftercare tips. Rachael’s piercing Instagram could be linked right there too.

2No opening hours anywhere on the site

What we saw

We didn’t find hours on the Home, Contact, Tattoos, Piercings or FAQ pages, or any note on walk-ins versus appointments. The Contact page has your address, phone and email and the line Have questions? Chat with us!!.

Why it matters

“Are they open now?” and “Do I need an appointment?” are usually the first two questions. Without them on the site, people have to call or look elsewhere.

Easy fix

Add your hours and a one-line walk-in/appointment policy to the Contact page and the footer.

3Different pages show different years in the footer

What we saw

The footer reads © 2021 - Alter Ego Tattoo on the Home and Contact pages, © 2020 - Alter Ego Tattoo on the Tattoos, Piercings and FAQ pages, and © 2019 - Alter Ego Tattoo on John’s artist page.

Why it matters

Older dates can make people wonder whether the rest of the information, like the artist list, is still current, especially when the dates disagree.

Easy fix

Set every page’s footer to the same text, “© 2026 Alter Ego Tattoo & Piercing, Knoxville”.

4Artist pages have names but no bios, and leftover page addresses

What we saw

The Our Team page lists Rachael Julian, James Paul, Lexi Schaaf, John Basilio and George Tackett with no text beyond their names. The artist pages use leftover template addresses: John is at /copy-of-andrew, George at /copy-of-daniel, Lexi at /copy-of-victoria-2 and Rachael at /copy-of-victoria.

Why it matters

Most people choose a tattoo artist, not just a shop. A sentence on each artist’s style helps them pick, and addresses with other people’s names look unfinished when shared.

Easy fix

Add two or three lines per artist (style, specialties, years tattooing) and rename the page addresses to /john, /george and so on.

5The home page says very little about the shop

What we saw

The home page text is ALTER EGO TATTOO and Est. 2001, plus the street address 7013 Kingston Pike without the city. “Knoxville” doesn’t appear in its visible text, the page has no meta description, and its images have no descriptions.

Why it matters

Someone landing on the home page from a search or a friend’s link doesn’t get a quick sense of what you do, where you are or how to book. Search engines rely on that text too.

Easy fix

Add a short intro under the logo (tattoos and piercings in West Knoxville since 2001), your full address with city, and a clear “Call (865) 330-0006” button.

What's working

  • Real history. Est. 2001 is right on the home page. Twenty-five years on Kingston Pike is something most shops can’t say.
  • An honest FAQ. Answers like Our minimum is $100 and goes up from there. and Some bad tattoos cannot be fixed. set clear expectations, which builds trust.
  • Your own domain and real links. alteregoknox.org is easy to share, your Facebook and Instagram are linked, and each artist’s photo links to their own Instagram so people can see current work.
