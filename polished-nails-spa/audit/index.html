Polished Nails Spa

We read through your Wix site on November 18, 2026, the way someone looking for a new nail salon in West Knoxville would. Your Services page lists every service with a clear price, right down to how long each lash appointment takes. Here are a few things that would help the site work as hard as you do.

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site today. Most are quick fixes.

1Your Facebook and Instagram buttons open Wix's pages, not yours

What we saw

On the Contact page, the Facebook and Instagram icons (in both the Contact Us and Get in Touch sections) link to facebook.com/wix and instagram.com/wix.

Why it matters

Nail clients love to scroll through photos of past sets before booking. Anyone who taps these icons ends up on Wix's own accounts instead of your work.

Quick fix

In the Wix editor, click each social icon and paste in the address of your own Facebook and Instagram pages, or remove the icons for now.

2Two different email addresses on the Contact page

What we saw

The page lists polishednails.salon865@gmail.com twice, but the clickable email in the Get in Touch section is polishednail.salon@gmail.com (no “s” and no “865”).

Why it matters

If the second address isn't yours, anyone who taps that link to ask about an appointment may be writing to an inbox you never see.

Quick fix

Change the Get in Touch email to polishednails.salon865@gmail.com so every link goes to the same inbox.

3The phone number can't be tapped to call, and there's no booking button

What we saw

865-692-1420 appears on every page as plain text, not a call link. We didn't find a “Book” or “Call now” button anywhere; the only buttons on the home page are the newsletter Join and the Wix banner's Start Now.

Why it matters

Most people looking up a salon are on their phone. If calling means memorizing or copying the number, some will just try the next salon in the search results.

Quick fix

Turn the number into a tel: link and add a “Call to book” button near the top of the home page.

4A Wix ad banner sits at the top of every page

What we saw

The first thing on each page is a Wix ad (This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. on the Contact page), and the footer reads Proudly created with Wix.com. The address is polishednailssalon.wixsite.com/polished-nails-spa.

Why it matters

The banner pushes your salon's name down and invites visitors to click away. A long wixsite address is also hard to put on a card or say out loud.

Quick fix

Connect your own domain (a short name like your salon's name) and remove the free-plan banner.

5Hours and street address are only on the Contact page

What we saw

The home page gives only Knoxville, Tn 37923 as a location and shows no hours. The Services page has no hours or address either. Both appear only on the Contact page: 116 North Peters Rd and Mon - Fri 9:30 am – 7:30 pm.

Why it matters

Visitors who land on the home page or the price list have to go hunting for “are they open now?” and “where exactly is it?”

Quick fix

Copy your hours and address (with a Google Maps link) into the footer so they show on every page.

6The footer says 2023, and a typo on the home page

What we saw

Every page ends with ©2023 by Polished Nails Spa. The home page's Get To Know Us section says With a wide selection of color, style, deigns, gel acrylics…

Why it matters

An old year can make people wonder if a site is still kept up, and small slips are what people notice when they're choosing between a few salons.

Quick fix

Update the year to 2026 and change “deigns” to “designs.”

What's working

  • Prices are up front. Your Services page lists pedicures, manicures, dip, enhancements, lashes, waxing and add-ons, each with a price, such as Basic Spa Pedicure at $37.
  • Lash times are listed. Showing how long each lash service takes (for example, Classic Full Set, 1h 30mins) helps clients plan their visit.
  • Your experience shows. Established In 2008 is right at the top of the home page, which builds trust with new clients.
