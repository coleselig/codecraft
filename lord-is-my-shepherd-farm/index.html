Our story

A family farm, five generations deep.

“We have been blessed to provide our family and friends with products from our farm for over 70 years.”

From the farm's own story

  • Established in 1953

    Family-operated for five generations near Cleveland, Tennessee.

  • A hatchery for endangered chickens

    Endangered chicken breeds hatched right here on the farm.

  • Members of The Livestock Conservancy

    Dedicated to safeguarding endangered livestock breeds.

  • Grown naturally

    No preservatives. Just the farm, the season and family know-how.
