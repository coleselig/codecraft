The farm
“It doesn’t get any fresher.”

Swallow Hill Blooms is a specialty cut flower farm rooted in a 230-year-old homestead in Jefferson County, Tennessee. Flowers aren’t shipped in from a wholesaler. They’re grown here, then picked on the farm within 24 hours of reaching you, as fresh as farm flowers get.
