Free website check-up for K. Wiens Photography

We looked at kwiensphoto.com (which forwards to kwiensphoto.wixsite.com/kwiensphotography) and its Sports, Portraits and Terms pages on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1The pages are almost all photos, with very little text

Saw The Sports and Portraits pages each have a one-word heading (sports, portraits) and about 30 photos with no descriptions (empty alt text). In the visible page text, Knoxville appears only once, in the footer line Katrina Wiens | Knoxville, TN.

Cost Google reads words, not pictures, so it has little to go on when someone searches for a Knoxville sports or portrait photographer.

Fix Add a short intro to each page (who you shoot, where, what a session includes) and a few words of description on the photos.

2No contact page, booking button or session info

Saw The menu is HOME, PORTFOLIO (SPORTS, PORTRAITS), PRIVATE and More. The pages we checked have no contact form, no booking button and no phone number. The only way to reach you is the kwiensphoto@gmail.com link in the footer.

Cost A parent or team who loves your photos has to guess what sessions you offer and write an email from scratch, and some will move on instead.

Fix Add a Sessions page listing the sports and portrait sessions you offer, and a Contact page with a short inquiry form and a clear "Book a session" button.

3A Wix ad sits at the top of every page

Saw This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button on the home page, and This site was designed with the .com website builder. Create your website today. with Start Now on Sports and Portraits.

Cost The first button a visitor sees sends them to Wix's sign-up page instead of to you.

Fix Move to a site without the ad banner and put "Book a session" in that spot.

4The Terms page still has template blanks

Saw The Terms & Conditions / Return Policy page is headed Tems & Conditions and still contains [INSERT LINK TO YOUR PRIVACY POLICY] and [INSERT LINK TO YOUR REFUND POLICY].

Cost This is the page people read before ordering prints, and unfilled blanks there can make them hesitate.

Fix Fix the heading typo and either fill in the two links or remove those lines.

What's working

Your work speaks for itself: roughly 30 sports photos and 30 portraits are already up, you own kwiensphoto.com, and the footer icons go to your real Facebook, Twitter, YouTube and Instagram (instagram.com/katrina_wiens). Your email and Knoxville, TN are on every page. That's a great base to build on.
