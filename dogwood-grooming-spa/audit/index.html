Dogwood Grooming Spa

We read through dogwoodgroomingspa.com on November 17, 2026, page by page, the way a pet owner looking for a groomer would. The site has a warm, family feel and a really thorough FAQ. Here are a few small things that would make it easier for new customers to book.

Checked November 17, 20266 things to tidy upNo cost, no strings

What we found

Each item below is something we saw ourselves on the live site. Most are quick fixes.

1Your hours are only listed inside the FAQ

What we saw

The FAQ page answers What are your hours? with Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM, last appointment at 4:30 PM. We did not see hours on the Home, Services, Contact Us, or Schedule Your Appointment pages.

Why it matters

“Are they open?” is one of the first things people check. If they can't find it quickly, many will call another groomer instead of digging through 26 FAQ questions.

Quick fix

Add a short hours line (Mon–Fri 9–5, last appointment 4:30) to the footer so it shows on every page, and on the Contact Us page.

2The “Covid Policy” link dials your phone

What we saw

On the Services, Contact Us and Schedule pages, a link labeled Covid Policy goes to tel:8653070306, so tapping it on a phone starts a call instead of opening a policy.

Why it matters

A visitor who taps it expecting to read something will be surprised by a phone call starting. It can feel like a mistake on the site.

Quick fix

Either point it to a short policy page, or (if the policy is no longer needed) remove the link.

3Booking sends people to a separate JotForm page

What we saw

The Schedule Your Appointment Now! page has a plain link to form.jotform.com/242576929370063, plus a call-to-schedule link. The form isn't on your site itself.

Why it matters

Each extra hop on a phone is a chance to lose someone, and a jump to a different-looking website can make people less sure they're in the right place.

Quick fix

Embed the JotForm directly on the Schedule page (JotForm gives an embed code), and add the FAQ's tip that you prefer bookings two weeks ahead right above it.

4No prices or price ranges on the pages we checked

What we saw

The Home, Services, Schedule and FAQ pages describe your grooming services but don't give prices or “starting at” ranges.

Why it matters

Pet owners comparing groomers usually want a rough idea of cost before they call. Even a range helps them choose you instead of moving on.

Quick fix

Add “starting at” prices by size (small / medium / large / cat), with a note that the final price depends on coat and condition.

5The Contact page only has a phone number and directions

What we saw

The Contact Us page shows 865-307-0306 and a “Get Directions” link to 1013 Leon Drive. There's no hours, email, map, or message form.

Why it matters

Some customers would rather send a quick message than call during the workday, especially with a question like “can you take my anxious dog?”

Quick fix

Add your hours, a simple message form (or email address), and an embedded Google Map.

6The footer looks a little out of date

What we saw

The footer reads @ 2024 Dogwood Grooming Spa LLC. followed by a stray !!. It uses “@” instead of the copyright sign, and the year is 2024.

Why it matters

Small details like an old year can make visitors wonder if the site (and the info on it) is still current.

Quick fix

Change it to “© 2026 Dogwood Grooming Spa LLC” (or set it to update the year automatically) and delete the “!!”.

What's working

  • Calling is easy. Your phone number is a tap-to-call link on every page we checked.
  • The FAQ is genuinely helpful. 26 real questions, from drying cages to sedation to fearful pets, which builds a lot of trust.
  • Your story comes through. The family-owned business and 18+ years of grooming experience are front and center.
