The farm

Small farm, busy bees.

Forty-five acres in Northeast Greene County, kept by a veteran-owned family. The bees work the wildflowers all season long, and every so often an unusual honey shows up early in the year.

Wildflower honey

Harvested through the growing season and sold as 100% pure raw local honey.

Beeswax

From the same hives as the honey.

Wild harvested

The farm lists its practices as organic (not certified), GMO-free and wild harvested.
