What we look for

The problems we keep finding on local sites.

These all came up on real small-business sites around East Tennessee while we were building concept sites. Most take minutes to fix once you know they're there.

Buttons that go nowhere

“Book Now”, “Free Estimates” and contact buttons that point to a dead page, an old site or just reload the home page.

Leftover template text

Sample text the website builder started with: “Lorem ipsum”, “I'm a product”, “Service Name $19.99”, a San Francisco address or a 123-456-7890 phone number.

Mixed-up details

Hours, phone numbers, emails or even business names that don't match between your site, your booking page and Google.

Social icons pointing at the wrong account

Facebook and Instagram icons that still open the website builder's own pages instead of yours.

Hard to call or book from a phone

Phone numbers that can't be tapped, booking links people have to copy and paste, hours hidden on a back page.

Signs a site looks abandoned

An old © year, a free-plan ad banner across the top, last season's specials still up.
