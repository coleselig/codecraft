Free website check-up for Reynolds's Landscaping

We looked at reynoldslandscapin1.wixsite.com/reynoldsslandscaping (the home page and every link on it) on Nov 18, 2026. Everything below is quoted from the live site that day.

What we found

1Your Facebook link goes back to your own site

Saw The intro shows the text https://www.facebook.com/Reynolds-landscaping-106071201196994/, but tapping it opens reynoldslandscapin1.wixsite.com/reynoldsslandscaping#homeservices instead of Facebook. (The two small Facebook icons do work.)

Cost People who tap that link to see your photos and reviews end up where they started.

Fix Point that link at your Facebook page, or remove the text link and keep the icons.

2Some services in the intro aren't on the page

Saw The intro says Our professional services offers lawn care ,landscaping,Tree service, pressure washing, painting., but the service sections cover only basic lawn care (cut, edge, trim), MULCHING & BED CLEANUPS. and LEAF & GUTTER CLEANOUTS!

Cost A customer looking for tree work, pressure washing or painting can't see what you do or whether to call you for it.

Fix Add a short section for each service you offer, with a photo, and tidy the spacing in that sentence.

3The phone number isn't tap-to-call

Saw (423) 863-5076 appears as plain text, while reynoldslandscaping20@yahoo.com is a clickable link.

Cost On a phone, a customer has to copy the number by hand to call you, and some won't bother.

Fix Make the number a tap-to-call link and add a "Call" button that stays on screen on phones.

4A Wix ad sits at the top of the page

Saw This website was built on Wix. Create yours today. with a Get Started button that links to Wix.

Cost The first button a visitor sees sends them to Wix's sign-up page instead of to you.

Fix Move to a site without the ad banner and put "Call for a free quote" in that spot.

5The footer still says ©2020

Saw ©2020 by Reynolds's landscaping. Proudly created with Wix.com

Cost A six-year-old date can make people wonder whether you're still in business.

Fix Show the current year and replace the Wix line with your phone number and service area.

What's working

Your phone, email and service area (Kingsport, Tri-Cities and surrounding areas) are on the page, your main services each have their own section, and both Facebook icons go to your real page. That's a solid base to build on.
