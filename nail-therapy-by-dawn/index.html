Why book here

Part artist, part therapist.

Her own job title says it best. Here’s what that looks like in the chair.

A maker of tiny art

Accent nails, ombre and full nail art sit right on the menu, from $10 to $20, on top of builder gel, Gel X, dip and acrylic sets.

Nails, lashes & brows

Classic, hybrid, wet set and volume lash extensions, lash lifts, brow lamination, henna brows and waxing. One appointment book for all of it.

Real spa extras

The Spoil Me treatments include hot paraffin wax, sugar scrub, massage and a hot towel. There’s a Callous Therapy pedicure for tired feet too.

Little ones welcome

Kids 10 and under get their own menu: manicures from $20, gel manis and pedicures from $25. The patience of a saint, remember?
